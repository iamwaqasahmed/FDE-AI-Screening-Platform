# FDE Programs Syllabus

## Program Description

### About the Program

The FDE Training Program combines technical engineering, AI architecture, product development, consulting, and project implementation.

Unlike a conventional classroom-based bootcamp, the program is structured around continuous project delivery:

- Weeks 1–4: Participants complete an end-to-end government AI implementation case study.

- Weeks 5–6: Participants work in teams on a real client or government-sponsored project using the Forward Deployed Engineering model.

The proposed program will train 100 government employees across four cohorts, with approximately 25 participants per classroom. The small-cohort model will provide sufficient access to instructors, technical mentors, project supervisors, and client stakeholders.

### Key Focus Areas

The FDE Program is designed for government employees who will contribute to AI transformation and digital implementation initiatives. The program develops four integrated capability areas:

1. Applied AI and Full-stack Engineering

2. Enterprise AI architecture and governance

3. Product and consulting capabilities

4. Real-world project implementation

### Learning Objectives & Outcome

The program is not intended to produce specialists in every AI or software discipline within six weeks. Its purpose is to develop implementation-oriented professionals with sufficient breadth, practical judgment, and delivery experience to lead or support enterprise AI projects.

The intended outcome is a national pool of government professionals who can support AI transformation initiatives from problem discovery through solution design, prototyping, implementation planning, and executive presentation.

- Business & AI Opportunity Discovery

  - Identify business problems, evaluate AI opportunities, and prioritize high- impact use cases.

- Enterprise AI & Data

  - Understand enterprise data, AI technologies, and select appropriate AI approaches.

- AI Solution Development

  - Build AI proof-of-concepts, integrate enterprise systems, and deploy AI applications.

- AI Architecture & Governance

  - Design secure, scalable, and responsible enterprise AI solutions.

- Product & Project Delivery

  - Define requirements, manage agile delivery, and plan the path from prototype to production.

- Consulting & Stakeholder Management

  - Facilitate discovery workshops, communicate with stakeholders, and present executive recommendations.

- Forward Deployed Engineering Practice

  - Deliver real client projects through multidisciplinary teamwork and practical implementation.

### What Makes This Course Different?

Most AI courses teach how to build an agent. However, this program teaches an FDE how to make an AI system reliable, economical, observable, secure, and deployable inside an enterprise.

## Target Audience

The program is intended for government employees who currently contribute, or are expected to contribute, to AI, data, digital transformation, technology, innovation, product, or operational improvement initiatives.

Suitable participant profiles may include:

- AI and machine learning specialists

- Data scientists and data engineers

- Software engineers

- Solution and enterprise architects

- Technical product managers

- Technology consultants

A multidisciplinary cohort is recommended. FDE teams are most effective when they combine technical, product, business, architecture, and domain expertise.

## 3. Admission and Selection Criteria

### Minimum Eligibility Criteria

Applicants should normally meet the following requirements:

- Saudi government employee or nominee from an approved public-sector entity

- Bachelor’s degree or equivalent professional experience

- 2 to 3 years of relevant professional experience

- Working proficiency in English, particularly for technical materials

<!-- Original PDF page 4 -->

- Ability to attend the full 6-week program approved by the line manager

- Nomination from the applicant’s employer or sponsoring entity

- Demonstrated interest in AI implementation, digital transformation, or technology- enabled service improvement

### Recommended Technical Readiness

Applicants should possess familiarity with several of the following:

- Programming or scripting

- Databases

- APIs and web applications

- Cloud computing fundamentals

- Machine learning or generative AI

- Product or project delivery experience

Not every participant must have a software engineering background. However, each cohort should contain a sufficient number of technically capable participants to support team-based solution development.

If a strong candidate misses some prerequisite, there’s an option for them to complete an online self-paced prep course.

### Assessment Process

The admission process may include:

1. Government entity nomination

2. Online application

3. Background and motivation questionnaire

4. Technical readiness assessment

5. English-language assessment, where required

6. Structured interview

7. Final review and cohort allocation

## Program Structure

The program consists of two integrated phases.

<!-- Original PDF page 6 -->

### Phase One: Applied AI Engineering & FDE Foundations

Participants develop the technical competencies required to design, build, deploy, and operate production-ready enterprise AI solutions through a structured government or enterprise AI implementation project. Using a guided case study, participants progressively build an end-to-end AI solution while learning the engineering practices and delivery methodologies expected of a Forward Deployed Engineer.

Duration: 4 Weeks (35 hours per week)

Technical Focus Areas

- AI solution design and enterprise architecture

- Model customization using prompt engineering, RAG, fine-tuning, and domain adaptation

- AI application engineering and enterprise systems integration

- Agent engineering and agentic workflow orchestration

- Model deployment, inference, and serving

- LLM, RAG, and agent evaluation with end-to-end testing

- Production operations, observability, and monitoring

- Token optimization, performance tuning, and cost management

- AI security, governance, and responsible AI

- Enterprise delivery methodologies and implementation best practices

This phase ensures that participants acquire the required concepts and delivery methods before engaging with a real client.

### Phase Two: Real Client Project (FDE POC)

Duration: 2 Weeks (35 hours per week)

Participants are assigned to a real government, Saudi industry, technology partner, or approved international client project.

During this phase, participants will:

- Meet client stakeholders

- Confirm the business and operational challenges and opportunities

- Define a limited and achievable project scope

- Assess available data and systems

- Design the solution

- Build or refine a proof of concept

- Validate the solution with users

- Present recommendations and next steps

Each project will be supervised by experienced FDE mentors, technical specialists, and project leads.

## Detailed 6-Week Syllabus

The program will develop participants across five competency domains.

1. AI Technologies & Engineering

  - Enterprise AI, LLMs, AI agents, data, application development, and deployment.

2. Enterprise AI Architecture

  - Secure, scalable, and governed AI systems across cloud, on-premises, and hybrid environments.

3. Product Development

  - Product strategy, user discovery, agile delivery, prototyping, and AI-assisted development.

4. Business & Consulting

  - Problem-solving, stakeholder engagement, executive communication, and AI adoption planning.

5. Project Implementation

  - End-to-end AI project delivery from discovery and solution design to proof of concept and production readiness.

Each week is organized around five focused training days. During the first four weeks, participants progressively develop an enterprise AI case study. During the final two weeks, they apply the same methodology to a Real Client Project.

6. Real Client Project Model
Project Sources
Projects may be sourced from:

   ●   Saudi ministries
   ●   Saudi government authorities
   ●   Public-sector organizations
   ●   Government-owned companies
   ●   Saudi technology and AI partners
   ●   Existing BeamData clients

Priority should be given to the Saudi government and public-sector use cases where suitable
projects, stakeholders, and data can be secured before the start of the program.

International client projects may be used as a supplementary option when they offer strong
learning value and can be completed within the required timeframe.

Project Selection Criteria
Each Real Client Project should:

   ●   Address a genuine organizational problem
   ●   Have an accessible client sponsor
   ●   Be achievable within a 2-week to 4-week proof-of-concept cycle
   ●   Have clearly defined users and intended outcomes
   ●   Provide sufficient data or representative sample data
   ●   Avoid uncontrolled access to classified or highly sensitive information
   ●   Permit participants to demonstrate an appropriate project outcome

- Have potential for continued implementation after the program

### Project Preparation

Projects should be identified and prepared before the training begins.

Preparation activities include:

- Client qualification

- Project sponsor confirmation

- Initial scope definition

- Data availability assessment

- Security and confidentiality review

- Technology and environment readiness

- Mentor assignment

- Agreement on expected deliverables

- Scheduling of client meetings and demonstrations

### Participant Team Structure

A cohort of 25 participants may be divided into approximately five teams of five participants.

Each team should include complementary roles such as:

- FDE team lead

- AI or data engineer

- Application or integration engineer

- Product manager/analyst

- Architecture, governance, or client engagement lead

Roles may rotate during the program to broaden participant experience.
