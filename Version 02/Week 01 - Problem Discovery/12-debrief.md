## What Week 2 will break

The week 2 drop changes the registry extract. `HOME_CLINIC` becomes `REGISTERED_SITE`. Dates of birth arrive as ISO dates only. A batch of registry numbers is reissued. Profiling code that hardcodes a column name, a date format, or a registry number will silently drop people. Keep the code that reads the files.

Week 2 is [Enterprise data](<../Week 02 - Enterprise Data/README.md>). Open `drops/week-2` at the start of that week.
