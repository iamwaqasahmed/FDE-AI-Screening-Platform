## Read these two, and nothing else

The board paper is [board-paper-2026-09-ai-screening](../data/corpus/board-papers/board-paper-2026-09-ai-screening.md). It is the sponsor's request. There is also a PDF original next to it. The Markdown is not the whole paper.

The first interview with the Chief Digital Officer is [interview-cdo-first-pass](../data/corpus/transcripts/interview-cdo-first-pass.md). It is a weak interview: the questions never leave the opening line. Read it as a warning, not as a discovery.

## Stop. Write the ask log.

One page, in [the ask-log template](templates/ask-log.md), before you open a spreadsheet.

- **Ask, verbatim.** Their words, from the paper and the opening line. Do not improve them.
- **What they think the work is.** The deliverable the paper is asking you to produce.
- **What a weak team does next.** The actions this request is designed to trigger.
- **Who you still need to hear from.** Roles, from the twelve persona cards. Not a solution.
- **What you will refuse to propose on Friday.** Risks, not a design.

The sponsor offered you a paper and an opening line. The people who run the pathway have not been asked a follow-up yet.

**Finish.** The ask log is one page. The extracts stay closed.
