# Retrieval card

## 1. Loss table

Ten baseline failures, and the reason for each: draft, superseded, wrong site, table only in the original, forwarded copy.

## 2. Baseline rates

Keyword overall percent. TF-IDF overall percent.

## 3. What the index stores

Status, effective date, sunset, site, on every chunk.

## 4. Binding

How a draft, a sunset, and a site overlay are applied.

## 5. Originals

PDF, DOCX, XLSX, and scan counts. Which facts are not in the Markdown.

## 6. Forwards

How many, and which document id you cite.

## 7. Refusals

The questions you refused, and the document that makes the refusal.

## 8. Instructions

The four sources, and that none of them was followed.

## 9. What moved a slice

Each change you kept, and the slice it moved. A change that moved nothing is not listed.

## 10. Not covered

Graph retrieval. Any embedding you tried and the slice it did not move.
