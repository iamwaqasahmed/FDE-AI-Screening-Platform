# Write the handoff

**Day 5. Consultant, product manager, and software engineer.** Write these before you open the [debrief](16-debrief.md). Use the templates. The checker looks for key facts, not for a heading style. Name dates as `YYYY-MM-DD`.

Both pages name the same sites, the ones the Week 3 memo allows, and the same earliest go-live, the one from note 03. A page that adds a hospital site, or a different date, does not match the other page.

## The executive page

Save [the executive template](templates/executive-page.md) as `executive-page.md`. The reader is the Chief Digital Officer.

Say that the clinical director is named in the week 6 memo, that the owners register is blank, and that the pilot stays stopped until every condition is confirmed. Name the sites. Name the site that does not join, and say it needs a data protection decision. Give the earliest date and the requested date, and say the requested date does not work.

These sentences fail the page on their own:

- "The pilot may start."
- "The owners register names the owner."
- "2027-02-01 is achievable."
- "No clinical safety owner exists."

The date in the third sentence is the requested date on this seed. On another seed, write that seed's requested date the same way, as a date that is not achievable. Do not write the sentence.

## The clinical page

Save [the clinical template](templates/clinical-page.md) as `clinical-page.md`. The reader is the clinical director. The ophthalmology lead is consulted, not the addressee.

The same sites. The same date. A grader signs every result. The model output is advisory. The hazard log is unsigned. No condition is confirmed. The pilot stays stopped.

These sentences fail the page on their own:

- "The model decides the result."
- "The pilot may start."
- "Every condition is met."

## The runbook

Save [the runbook template](templates/runbook.md) as `runbook.md`. Someone who was not in the build has to operate this, including the part where they do not start it.

Name who to call: the programme manager for operations, the information security officer for an incident, the clinical director to suspend the system. A possible patient-harm incident is reported within four hours. The service will not diagnose, will not send a letter, and stays stopped. The discovery token expires on the date from note 09. It is not valid for production.

Do not write "Start the pilot." Do not write "The discovery token is valid for production."

**Finish.** The two pages name the same sites and the same date. The runbook tells an operator who to call and what not to do.
