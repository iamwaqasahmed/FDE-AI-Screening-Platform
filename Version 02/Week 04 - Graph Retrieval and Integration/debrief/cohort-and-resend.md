# Debrief — cohort and re-send

**The chase list.** 8,142 local ids, which is Week 2's latest referral with a blank seen date. It is not the overdue cohort. The hidden count of people actually overdue is 1,677, and it is still not in the drop.

Of the 8,142: 268 were already seen in a clinic letter, 270 are flagged deceased, and 209 are flagged moved. The bound that remains is 7,395, the same upper bound as Week 2. Inside it, 5,337 were reached once, 1,789 were printed twice, and 269 were not reached.

**C-07.** 269 local ids, and 269 people. It is still an upper bound. The hidden count, lost chase letters to people who really are overdue, is 57. Quoting 269 as that hidden count fails.

**Pilot scope.** 10 of those local ids are also in the Week 3 pilot scope. Still an upper bound.

**The clock.** 71 of the 269 have a local specialist clock. The documents are `site-sop-ncec-2024` and `site-sop-harbour-2025`. The median days past that clock, on 2026-10-26, is 248. A blank hospital number is not a hospital. 2,133 referral rows have no hospital number. No hospital number maps to two hospitals.

**The re-send.** 335 lost requests. 303 are re-sent. 12 are held because the person was already seen, 14 because the flag is deceased, 6 because the flag is moved. The file is keyed by the original request id. It has no name and no date of birth.

**The agreement.** Clause 1 of `dsa-postright-2020` covers invitation and result letters. It does not cover a chase letter. The data protection officer has not decided. Retention is 90 days, so the evidence deadline for a letter dispatched on 2026-10-25 is 2027-01-23. The zone is processor. The region is UK.

**The window.** FRZ-0302 runs 2026-12-14 to 2027-01-08. The next letters window is 2026-11-04. The last before the freeze is 2026-12-02. The first after it is 2027-02-03. A re-send does not need a release. A new template does.
