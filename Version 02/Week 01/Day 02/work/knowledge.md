# Knowledge

1. Retrieval fits better than fine-tuning for `p-info`. Hours, preparation, and required documents change by site and by version. The answer is a document that is in force for that reader.

2. A model is the wrong tool for `p-clock`. The dashboard names the column `booking_date`. The fix is the query. `p-slots` is the same kind of decision: a workflow, not a sentence that holds a slot.
