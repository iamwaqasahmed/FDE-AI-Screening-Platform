## Knowledge record

Introduced and practised: Today the medical director's rules, the scenario slice, and the tool catalog arrive. You are one person, and you do all five parts of the job yourself. No part is handed to someone else. You do four things, in this order: 1. You read the approval rules, the routing list, the tool catalog, and the scenario slice. The pages are in this folder. 2. You run `wf-self-service`. A patient can ask for status and book a slot. A return cannot leave without a coordinator. 3. You keep the trace the runner writes, and you write a one-page agent note. 4. You answer two questions: when a second agent is worth its tokens, and what must be true before a slot is booked. At the end, two checks run the agent again and read the agent note and the knowledge file. The trainer reads the sentences in `knowledge.md`. On that file the checks only require two numbered lines with a word after the number. They do not grade those sentences. They do grade the trace and the note.
