# Week 1 Day 2

The leaders' numbers.

## Knowledge record

Introduced and practised: Today the leaders' numbers arrive. You are still not building the assistant. You are one person, and you do all five parts of the job yourself. No part is handed to someone else. You do four things, in this order: 1. You read the nine problems and the pages of figures. A figure on those pages is a claim until you measure it. 2. You run four baselines. Each one is a simple count or score. You keep the printed lines. 3. You write an approach memo. Every problem gets a family, a measured cell, a citation, a system, and a reason. 4. You write a draft brief. It marks claims, sizes every problem, and ranks the nine. At the end, two checks look at the memo and the brief. A short `knowledge.md` file holds your own answers. The trainer reads that file. The checks do not.
