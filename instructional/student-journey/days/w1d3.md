# Week 1 Day 3

The intake workflow.

## Knowledge record

Introduced and practised: Today the event stream, the SLA policy, and the business calendar arrive. You are one person, and you do all five parts of the job yourself. No part is handed to someone else. You do four things, in this order: 1. You read the stream, the policy, the calendar, and the event contract. The pages are in this folder. 2. You run `wf-intake` from `referral.submitted` to a held approval. The workflow does not accept, return, or book. 3. You keep the scenario log the worker writes, and you write a one-page intake note. 4. You answer two questions: which clock starts an SLA, and what a second delivery of the same event should do. At the end, one check runs the worker again and reads the intake note and the knowledge file. The trainer reads the sentences in `knowledge.md`. On that file the check only requires two numbered lines with a word after the number. It does not grade those sentences. It does grade the worker and the note.

Assumed from earlier days: the background recap above this section in `00-background.md`. The carry-forward from the previous solution knowledge chapter is the same result.

What remains difficult: The working week counts Friday and Saturday as the weekend. A second event id must not open a second approval.

Dependencies: the release this day's setup builds, plus any earlier pack the setup chapter names. The checks grade `work/` after `prepare_work.py` and the chapter pastes.
