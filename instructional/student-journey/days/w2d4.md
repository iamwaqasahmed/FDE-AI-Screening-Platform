Deploy.

## Knowledge record

Introduced and practised: Today the deployment standard and the prod credentials arrive. You leave with a private-cloud URL, a health probe that reports the registered hash, and `deployment.md`. Booking from chat still waits. You are one person, and you do all five parts of the job yourself. No part is handed to someone else. You do five things, in this order: 1. You read the deploy notice, the standard, the register, the credentials, and the two lines the deploy demo prints. The pages are in this folder. 2. You sequence the sprint so the deploy comes before the readout. 3. You replace the image admission, the probe, the router, the credentials, the manifest, and the work pipeline. The probe reports the registered hash. The image account is not root. Prod reads the prod credential. 4. You map the URL onto `zone-private`, write the operator steps, and fill the deployment note. 5. You answer two questions: which zone this URL is in, and what a rollback changes. At the end, one check runs. It loads the app in `work/app` and reads the pages. A miss says the probe does not report the registered hash, the image runs as root, a national id is in the deploy log, prod uses the dev credential, the router leaves the private endpoint, or the deployment note is missing a required fact. It can also say the manifest is not in the team namespace, the health probe is missing, the pipeline is not in CI, the url is not in zone-private, the operator steps are missing, or the sprint does not book the readout after the deploy. It does not name a hash, a token, the quota, or a national id.
