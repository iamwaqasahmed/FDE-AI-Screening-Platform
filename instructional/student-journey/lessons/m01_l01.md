# Module 1, Lesson 1

Arrival.

## Knowledge record

Introduced and practised: Today is the arrival. You are not building the assistant. You are one person, and you do all five parts of the job yourself. You do four things, in this order: 1. You learn who asked, what they asked, and which approval gate you are standing in. 2. You file an access request for the files and the dev credential this day needs. The cluster does not give you its full history today. 3. You keep a tiny pipeline green. It only answers "I am up." Its real job is to reject a secret or a patient letter if one is copied into your work. Coding assistants are allowed. Patient data and secrets are not. 4. You interview two of the cluster's people. They answer the question you ask. They do not volunteer the constraint that would change the design. At the end, two checks look at your pipeline and your two interviews. A short `knowledge.md` file holds your own answers. The trainer reads that file. The checks do not.
