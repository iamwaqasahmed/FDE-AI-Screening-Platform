# Recall letter template

Status: In force.
Template id: letter-recall

Use this when a person has no completed result, or the photo could not be read and they need another capture. Do not state that disease is present or absent.

---

Dear {{forename}} {{surname}},

Local id: {{local_pid}}
Date of birth: {{dob}}
Clinic: {{clinic}}

We do not yet have a completed diabetic eye screen for you. Please contact {{clinic}} to arrange another photo. Bring this letter with you.

This letter is not a diagnosis.

Hartland Regional Health Authority
Eye screening service
---
