# Issued screening procedure

Status: In force. Effective 1 March 2019.

This procedure governs diabetic eye screening for Hartland Regional Health Authority. It is the current operating rule.

## Who is eligible

An eligible person is a row on the diabetes registry with no deceased or moved flag. Coverage is the share of eligible people who have a recorded result in the last 24 months.

A recorded result includes a readable grade and an ungradable grade. A photo with no result code is not a completed screen.

## How a result is recorded

The grader reads the photo and records one code from the grade-code list. The grader signs every result that changes what happens to the patient.

## Referral

The result letter has one referral box. The grader ticks that box when the patient should be sent to ophthalmology. The service does not keep a separate urgent list. Mild and sight-threatening disease use the same box and the same clinic queue.

## What this procedure does not do

This procedure sets no day target for a specialist visit. A newer urgent-pathway note exists as a draft. It is not in force. Do not apply it as current policy.
