# Executive page

For the Chief Digital Officer.

The quarter can show a working eye-screening service at three sites:

- Hill Counties Health Centre
- Oakridge Primary Care
- North City Eye Clinic

The coverage tool reports 84,120 eligible people and 34,490 completed screens. Those counts are for the authority. They are not split into a separate total per clinic.

The 93% figure is a hospital slide. It does not prove the primary-care pilot.
