# Week 1, Day 1 — Forward deployed engineering foundations

Facilitator only.

## Objective

The participant can describe the Al-Waha engagement in the cluster's words, and leaves a starter pipeline that deploys and that rejects a secret.

## Teach before the lab

The FDE loop, `pol-stage-gate` (G0–G5), claims versus measurements, and the assistant-use rules.

## Case

Kickoff with the sponsor's sentence. The team files an access request. Release `rel-w1d1`.

## Lab and trap

Clone the starter, point it at dev credentials, make CI green, and interview two personas. The trap is pasting a sample letter into a coding assistant.

## Check and demo

`chk-w1-repo`, `chk-w1-interview`. Run one interview turn on `practice`, then a scan that fails on a planted secret.
