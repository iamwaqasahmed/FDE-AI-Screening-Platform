# Module 1, Lesson 5 — Score the proof — Evaluation, architecture, and the readout

Facilitator only.

## Objective

The participant can score the proof of concept, publish the diagram and the design note, and defend them to the steering committee.

## Teach before the lab

Scenario grading, failure names, and what Stage 2 will have to revisit.

## Case

Steering questions `steer-m01`. Release `rel-m01-l05`.

## Lab and trap

Run the scenario set, including a timeout and `race-slot`. Finish `architecture.md` and `design.md`. Read out. The trap is a diagram with no human step, or a design that promises triage.

## Check and demo

`chk-agent`, `chk-design`, `chk-readout`, `chk-individual-m01`. Run the practice-world readout, answering as `cdo` from the card only.
