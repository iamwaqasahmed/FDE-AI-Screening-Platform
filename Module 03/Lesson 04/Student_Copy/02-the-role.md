# 02 — The role

[Chapter 00](00-background.md) said which release this is. This chapter is how you behave on the client's site for this release. Nothing is filed yet.

A forward deployed engineer works on the client's problem, inside the client's constraints, and leaves something the client can run and check. On this engagement the client is the Al-Waha Health Cluster. You are with the patient-access service. You hold all five parts yourself.

## The five parts today

| Part | What you will leave |
| --- | --- |
| Lead | The sprint: the score, then the failures, then the workshop, and the workshop stays on evidence |
| AI or data engineer | Extraction, citation, and journey against their budgets, a forecast of the later window, the four failure names, then the intervals and the experiment |
| Application engineer | A judge that follows the calibration label, a regression that rejects a fluent wrong citation, and an uncommented evaluation gate |
| Product manager | The workshop notes: the four seats, and the note that the dot-vote is not the value model |
| Architect | The benchmark line that the workflow failure is a design bug, and the two knowledge answers |

You do these in [chapter 05](05-the-sprint.md), [chapter 06](06-the-harness.md), [chapter 07](07-the-gate.md), [chapter 08](08-the-advanced.md), and [chapter 09](09-the-pages.md), in that order. [Chapter 10](10-finish.md) files the two answers and runs the two checks.

The checks do not read an interview. You do not run an interview command, and you do not write interview notes.
