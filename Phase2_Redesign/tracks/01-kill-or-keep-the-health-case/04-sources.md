Labels: **Proven** means this pass opened the page or PDF, unless a row says the fetch timed out and the wording is from a search-index extract. **Emerging** means announced, not in force, a vendor roadmap, or a secondary alert not matched to the Official Gazette. **Speculative** is not used here; curriculum judgments are marked in the other files.
