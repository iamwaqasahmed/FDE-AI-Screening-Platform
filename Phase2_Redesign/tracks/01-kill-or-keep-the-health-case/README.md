# Kill or keep the health case

Adversarial curriculum note. Research date: 30 October 2026.

This folder attacks the decision to keep the fictional Al-Waha Health Cluster as the spine of a long forward-deployed engineering course for Saudi government employees. Phase-1 notes in `Idea_CrossCutting/07-enterprise-simulation` said to keep that narrative and thicken it. The case itself (`case/README.md`) already sends serious deployment to a fictional private cloud and allows a public-cloud model only for content the cluster has classed as public. The new brief allows the enterprise to be replaced. It also requires Amazon Web Services as a serious implementation track, local Python on a laptop, and a real Personal Data Protection Law constraint on health data.

Nothing here edits course files, gold data, checks, or any `Idea_*` folder.

## Labels

- **Proven.** The sentence is in a page or PDF this pass opened, or in a search-index extract that this pack marks as unfetched.
- **Emerging.** Announced and not yet in force, a vendor roadmap, or a secondary professional alert this pass did not match to the Official Gazette.
- **Speculative.** A curriculum judgment. It is not a fact about a Saudi institution.

English PDFs from SDAIA and the Bureau of Experts are translations. The National Cybersecurity Authority says the Arabic text of the Cloud Cybersecurity Controls is the binding one. Decree numbers are not quiz items.

## Verdict

Replace Al-Waha as the spine. The winner is **Qitaf Agency**, a fictional facilities agency whose system of record is a Ministry of Finance procurement portal it does not own. The runner-up is **Shatt Municipality**, a fictional municipality on a Balady-shaped permit chain. A health hybrid (cluster plus payer plus national booking channel) concentrates health data and makes the cloud track less honest, so it loses. The win for Qitaf holds only while supplier quotations stay off the student AWS account. The classification policy's own examples put those quotations beside the individual health file. If a later build uploads them to make the cloud feel real, reject Qitaf as well.

Volumes, savings, and adoption rates are not invented in this pack. Where a source prints a figure, the figure stays that source's claim.
