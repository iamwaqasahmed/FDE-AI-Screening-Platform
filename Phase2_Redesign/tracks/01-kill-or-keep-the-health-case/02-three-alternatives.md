# Three alternative enterprises

Each enterprise below is fiction. Real institutions appear only as analogues the fiction must not impersonate. The student does not rebuild Etimad, Balady, Qiwa, Mudad, or GOSI, and does not call production identity, payment, or registry APIs. Local Python holds the grader, the rules baseline, and every fixture the classification examples treat as restricted. AWS, when it is honest, holds a narrower catalog the case has marked public.

Shared rules, all three:

- A release fails if the model role can read a national identifier, a wage, a quotation, a health file, or a sealed bid.
- The grader runs on the laptop. Moving the stop into a cloud function the reviewer cannot rerun offline fails the brief.
- No lesson teaches how to forge a seal, evade an inspection, decrypt a live portal, or construct a file that looks compliant.
- Article numbers are not quiz items. The June 2026 and September 2026 procurement texts disagree on committee structure, and the Arabic laws control.

## 1. Qitaf Agency

Fictional facilities and works agency. Analogue: a government agency that must run competitions through the Ministry of Finance procurement portal and may not replace it.

### Organizations

Qitaf's procurement department, a proposal committee, the official who alone may award, synthetic bidders, and a finance reviewer. Offstage, simulated as stubs: the Portal (the 2019 law's electronic portal, which the Ministry of Finance creates and oversees), and a seat for the Local Content and Government Procurement Authority, which the 2019 law already names and which a 21 October 2026 Baker McKenzie alert says the newer law strengthens. **Emerging:** that alert. This pass did not match it to the Official Gazette.

Officers sign in through a Nafath-shaped stub. The National Platform describes Nafath as SDAIA's unified access for government and private services, in Arabic and English. Direct fetch of that page timed out on 30 October 2026; the description here is from the search-index extract of `https://my.gov.sa/en/services/119727`.

### The broken system the student inherits

The Portal is supposed to publish the competition, keep proposals encrypted, and hold the agency's contract record. Articles 16 and 17 of the 2019 Government Tenders and Procurement Law (Royal Decree M/128, 16 July 2019, English translation hosted by the Ministry of Investment) say tenders go through the Portal, and that the Portal must provide the highest degree of privacy, confidentiality, safety, transparency, and integrity. Article 37 says proposals submitted through the Portal are encrypted. Article 44 says that where technical and financial proposals are separate, only the technical proposals are opened. Article 46 says a technical proposal that fails is disqualified and the financial proposal is returned intact. Article 45 says the review committee recommends, records dissent, and sends minutes to the person who has authority to award. The same article forbids one person to chair the review and hold the award, to chair both opening and review, or to sit on both committees.

A Ministry of Finance compilation whose header reads as the version of 5 June 2026 still defines the Portal as the single electronic procurement gate under the Ministry, still describes opening technical files without the financial files, and defines a standstill (`فترة التوقف`) that starts when bidders are told the winning offer so they can grieve. Baker McKenzie, 21 October 2026, says a new law was published on 4 September 2026, replaces the 2019 law, takes effect on 2 January 2027, and merges opening and evaluation into one committee, with implementing regulations still in draft. **Proven:** the 2019 translation and the June 2026 compilation as fetched. **Emerging:** the September law's committee merge, until the Gazette text is the thing being taught. The course opening on 10 January 2027 falls after that stated effective date. Teach the invariants that are in the texts this pass opened. Do not quiz which committee structure is in force that week.

What Qitaf actually has is a nightly export an intern has already unpacked into one shared folder, a spreadsheet whose weights do not match the published specification, an Arabic clause contradicted by an English attachment, and a pilot prompt that asks a model to recommend the best proposal. The award minutes have been drafted from that ranking.

### Why it is hard

Two systems of record are in conflict: the Portal, which the agency does not control, and the folder, which is where the pilot lives. The technical file and the financial file have to stay apart until the technical gate passes. Criteria are those published in the tender documents, so a private weight change is a different competition. Names announced on the Portal and names in the folder can diverge. Validity is ninety days from opening in the 2019 text, extendable once; a model that "just finishes the ranking" after that window is late. Arabic is the language of the law; the English rendering is a translation. A bilingual clash is planted as a fixture. **Speculative** as a claim about how often real bids arrive in English. **Proven** as a property of the official translation note.

Cost has units: committee hours, a document fee the Council of Ministers sets (the statute says a fee exists and does not fix the number here), model and storage charges, and the cost of an award that has to be rerun. Unknown cells stay blank. Inventing a savings rate is a failing sentence.

Identity is officer, delegate, and supplier. A conflict declaration sits in the fixture because the Baker alert says conflict rules were still to be issued. The sim does not invent those rules.

### What must never be automated

The award, the disqualification, the price negotiation the 2019 Article 47 assigns to the committee, and any waiver of a published criterion. The Ministry publishes the list of persons with whom dealing is prohibited. The model does not add a name to that list and does not emit a corruption score or a vendor reputation score. SDAIA's principles forbid social scoring. The Implementing Regulation requires explicit consent for a decision made solely by automated processing of personal data. A completeness check on a public specification is allowed. A ranking the committee may read as advice is the thing the release must be able to stop, because it is the thing the inherited pilot is already doing.

The June 2026 compilation states the law's purposes as regulating procedure, preventing influence and personal interest, protecting public funds, seeking value, and keeping the process transparent. **Speculative:** a scoring copilot is a practical path for that influence. The purpose text is **Proven** as a reading of that compilation. The copilot claim is the reason the stop exists.

### Is AWS an honest host?

Conditional yes, and only for objects in the public illustrative class: the published announcement, the published specification, the published rubric, the public question log, the deadline calendar. IAM roles, a key, a bucket policy, CloudTrail, a state machine for the completeness checklist, a queue for questions, a budget alarm, and logs are the implementation track. The model execution role has no read on the financial prefix. Proving that denial is the lab.

Supplier contracts, their quotations, and bid submissions are on the NDMO restricted examples, in the same illustrative list as the health file and the national ID. They stay in local Python as synthetic rows. A student account is not a government cloud tenant under CCC-2: 2024. An in-Kingdom Region on a December 2026 timetable does not establish compliance, and this pack does not assume Bedrock, or any named AI service, is present there in January 2027. ALLAM is not a dependency.

### What would be fake

Calling the account the Portal or Etimad. A Lambda that awards. Bahrain or Virginia described as the Kingdom. A regional service list copied from a blog instead of checked. An English-only rubric. An invented saving. A model that detects corrupt vendors. A lesson on breaking portal encryption. The inherited folder is already decrypted because an intern broke process. The student inherits the mess. The student is not taught the break.

## 2. Shatt Municipality

Fictional municipality. Analogue: an amana or municipality whose building permits are supposed to run through Balady, and which still runs a side desk for an old district.

### Organizations

The permit owner, a design engineering office, a supervising office, a contractor, a technical inspection company, an insurer, and municipal staff. The Ministry of Municipalities and Housing service card for issuing a building permit, fetched 30 October 2026, says the service is on the Balady platform. After a design is approved and fees are paid, permit details go to other parties through integrated services. The card names the electricity company, the water company, the Ministry of Energy, Civil Defense, the insurer, and the Real Estate Registry. Required inputs include an electronic title deed from the Ministry of Justice (or a housing or investment contract), a cadastral report, contracts with the design office, the supervising office, and the contractor, latent-defects insurance, and a soil study. The national platform in the sim is a stub. Shatt does not operate it.

### The broken system the student inherits

Shatt's old-town desk still accepts a paper survey decision. The cadastral number and the title-deed number do not join. Inspector comments arrive as exported chat. A permit PDF was printed before the insurance-paid flag. For applications after 25 June 2025 the ministry card says insurance documents are issued inside the Balady journey and that there is no other insurance path; older permits use a different contractor path. The side desk follows neither.

The card lists the service language as Arabic, the time required as 1–10 days, and the response time as 10 days. The sim keeps both clocks and does not "correct" the page. SMS exists. Payment exists. The student inherits a municipality that has automated the wrong step (printing the permit) and left the join broken.

### Why it is hard

The ministry's own steps are a delegation chain, not a form. The owner authorizes a design office. The design office submits to a technical inspection company that the insurer assigns. The inspector audits against the Saudi Building Code and can return the file. Municipal staff can return it again. Only then is a design-approval certificate issued. The contractor and the supervising office are named later. The insurer issues a policy after payment. The permit is not valid for use until the insurance payment is complete. Step 4 and step 8 already automate assignment and the insurance request. The human judgments are the code audit and the municipal review.

Identity is a chain of delegations, plus a national identifier on the owner and commercial registration on the offices. A Balady service extract describes login through Unified National Access (Absher). This pass fetched the ministry card and did not re-fetch that Balady page, so the Absher sentence stays an unfetched extract.

Arabic is the legal language of the service. A fixture may include an English CAD layer whose room names do not match the Arabic undertaking. That clash is planted. It is not a statistic about engineering offices.

### What must never be automated

The inspector's finding of compliance with the building code, the municipal approval, the design-approval certificate, and any status that reads as Civil Defense acceptance. Civil Defense is a recipient of permit details on this card, not an approver this pass documented. The Balady home page, in a search extract, lists a demolition permit as a separate service. This pass did not open its steps. Auto-issuing demolition or occupancy is banned because SDAIA's principles require a human final determination where the impact is irreversible or life-and-death. **Speculative** as a mapping from that sentence onto demolition. **Proven** as the ministry's assignment of code audit and municipal review to named roles, and as the rule that a fee payment does not make the permit valid before insurance is paid.

Routing to the designated inspector, the fee invoice, and the insurance-document request may be automated. The ministry card already describes those as automatic. A model that drafts a deficiency letter for a human to send is allowed. A model that clears the deficiency is not.

### Is AWS an honest host?

Weaker than Qitaf. The work object is the application: drawings, a title deed, owner identity, undertakings. Identity attributes are on the restricted examples. A status bus of case tokens, with no deed and no identifier, can live in the student account: queue, state machine, budget, trail. The application file stays local. That is a real but narrower cloud track, because the interesting bytes are the ones that must not be uploaded. New Regions and service availability have the same limits as in Qitaf.

### What would be fake

A clone of Balady. A permit issued by a function. An English user interface presented as the legal service. A webhook from "Civil Defense" that returns success. Homepage user counts or license counts copied off Balady. This pack did not use those counters. A single clock where the ministry page prints two.

## 3. Ajir Desk

Fictional regional labor-compliance desk. Analogues: the HRSD establishment record, the GOSI register, and the Mudad wage-file channel. The desk inspects. It does not operate those platforms.

### Organizations

Ajir inspectors, a synthetic establishment, its employees as rows, a bank as an external file source, and stubs for the establishment registry, the social-insurance register, and the wage-file channel. The HRSD service card "Uploading the Wage Protection file," fetched 30 October 2026, says an establishment uploads a sealed electronic file of pay and reconciliation data, the file is checked against social-insurance rules, and the compliance rate updates immediately. The channel is Mudad Business. The same page's steps say sign up on "Madad," and a later answer says wage files are uploaded only through the compliance system in Mudad, not through the ministry e-services site that used to take them. Prerequisites on the card: registration with the ministry, registration with the General Organization for Social Insurance, and bank accounts for the establishment and the employees. The card says the service language is Arabic and English, the fee is free, and the duration is immediate. It says the program launched in 2013.

An ILO information note, seen as a search extract and not re-paged as a PDF in this pass, describes Mudad as launched in December 2020 and prints a compliance ratio: committing employees over GOSI-registered employees plus employees who are not GOSI-registered. **Proven:** the HRSD card. **Emerging:** the ILO formula and the December 2020 date, until the PDF is re-opened. The sim may use a toy ratio that is labeled as a teaching stand-in. It must not present the ILO extract as the live formula.

DGA's public register lists Qiwa as the labor-system platform of the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development. Direct fetch of `https://dga.gov.sa/en/node/508` timed out. The license number in the search extract is omitted on purpose.

### The broken system the student inherits

For one worker the contract wage, the wage GOSI was given, and the wage in the bank file are three different numbers. The complaint is in Arabic. The desk's sheet uses the previous month. The establishment number on the file does not match the number on the registry. Someone has asked for a model that predicts which establishments "tend to violate," and has left a draft sanction letter in the folder.

### Why it is hard

The join is the product. Identity is a national ID or an iqama, tied to an establishment, tied to a bank account the desk does not hold. The official page disagrees with itself about the platform name. Processing is described as immediate, while files in the inherited tray are late. Justifications exist and have to be read by a person. Arabic and English are both declared service languages, so a Latin name on the bank row and an Arabic name on the contract are a planned fixture, not a curiosity.

The correct baseline is a rules join a student can write in Python and explain. A language model that "reconciles" the three wages by prose is the failure the eval should catch. That is good teaching and a poor match to a brief that also wants a serious cloud implementation, because the rows that make the join interesting are salaries.

### What must never be automated

The sanction, establishment closure, a work ban, a deportation recommendation, and the outcome of a labor dispute. A mismatch flag to an inspector is allowed. A score of how likely an establishment or a worker is to be a violator is social scoring and is banned. The Implementing Regulation's explicit-consent rule for solely automated decisions applies if the row identifies a person.

This course does not teach how to build, seal, or adjust a wage file. The student receives synthetic mismatched extracts. File layouts that would help someone pass a compliance check are out of scope.

### Is AWS an honest host?

Poor. Employee salaries are an NDMO restricted example. The work object is the wage. A cloud log of case tokens with amounts removed is an honest scrap of operations work and a thin implementation track. Local Python is the honest host. Choosing Ajir as the spine would make AWS a logo again, which is the failure this pack is trying to escape.

### What would be fake

"We deployed Mudad." A model that writes the violation. A tutorial in the wage-file format. Real payroll. A claim that the ILO ratio is the rate Ajir must hit. The ministry card says the rate updates immediately. It does not, on the page this pass opened, print the formula.

## How the three differ

| | Qitaf | Shatt | Ajir |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inherited break | One folder holds both proposal files; a pilot ranks a winner | Permit printed before insurance; deed and survey do not join | Three wages, one worker; a draft sanction |
| Public-class cloud object | Published specification and rubric | Case-token status, no deed | Case-token status, no wage |
| Human decision | Award, disqualification, negotiation | Code audit, municipal approval, safety and demolition | Sanction and any punitive status |
| AWS as the main track | Yes, if quotations never arrive | Partial | No |
| Laptop | Grader, sealed synthetic bids | Grader, application file | Grader and the join |

Social-security eligibility and customs risk selection were considered and are not proposed. The Social Security Law (Royal Decree M/32, published on the ministry's site) Article 6 requires the ministry to accept or reject, with reasons, within twenty days. A model that denies a pension is the decision that article assigns to the ministry, and it sits next to SDAIA's ban on social scoring. Fasah exists as a customs window; a risk model that releases or holds goods teaches the wrong operational habit and slides toward evasion. Neither gives a cleaner cloud split than Qitaf.
