# G — Ideas that stay off the core path

These are real enough to keep in a backlog. They are not gates. Each one failed the test “a mixed cohort can do this alone, on a laptop, without a fake claim of compliance,” or it repeats a gate that already exists.

## A second engagement after the gates

A port-community case: a bill of lading, a hold the assistant must not release, EDI and a file drop, a trucking window, Arabic and English documents. It teaches the same seams with a cleaner AWS story, because less of the payload is health data. It belongs after someone has finished the Access Office gates, as a transfer exam: same decisions, unfamiliar nouns. Building it into the first program resets the vocabulary for no new skill.

## A benefits-eligibility case

A score that recommends a review and never a denial. Strong ethics. The operational mess is thinner than referrals plus a contact center plus a file-only hospital. Use the pattern as a seminar, not a second simulator.

## Agents that browse a desktop

Vendor guidance for computer-use tools tells operators not to process health information that way. The refusal is a paragraph in gate 17. A lab that drives a desktop is out.

## Graph retrieval and multi-hop

Useful when the question is about a whole corpus. The Access Office questions are about one clause, one version, and one audience. An open hop can land on the unauthorized circular. Named in gate 15. Not built.

## Fine-tuning a 7B model, and vendor fine-tune APIs

Phase 1 found vendor docs that described a hosted fine-tune path as winding down for new users, and found no opened paper in which a reasoning model beat a small adapter on this kind of field extraction. Gate 14 already allows a tiny local update and a refusal. A GPU lab that downloads a base model is the extension under gate 23, and only for serving measurements the engine prints itself.

## A full service mesh, a Kafka cluster, a feature store

Each one can be the right production tool. Each one hides the invariant the gate is trying to show: an outbox, an idempotency key, a contract, a filter before score. They can appear as the “what you will meet” card. They are not installed.

## AWS Fault Injection Service, and chaos in a real account

The principle is right. The blast radius is wrong for this cohort and for patients who are fictional but whose patterns are still personal-data shaped. The graded drill stays on the simulator. FIS is a distinction after gate 32 for students who already have an account and a destroy habit.

## Simulated stakeholders driven by a live model

A model that plays the director would make interviews feel alive and would make the checker flaky. The cards stay scripted. Claims and facts stay in the gold table. A live persona is a research toy, not a gate.

## Arabic speech models

A locale code is not a dialect. Television corpora are not a phone line. The course teaches the channel and the wording. It does not train a recognizer.

## Paying a labeling vendor, and an online experiment on real patients

Out of scope. The grader holds the keys. The one comparison in gate 16 is pre-registered and its legal outcome on that file is no call.
