# 1 — Course vision

## Who it is for

Government staff who will support an AI implementation from the first conversation through a system that other people operate. They can read technical English. They have some experience with programming, data, APIs, or cloud. They are not assumed to be specialists in all of those. The language of the labs is Python 3.12. A GPU, a paid cloud account, and a live model are not required to finish.

The work is self-paced. Nothing in the course is a week, a cohort day, or an attendance rule.

## What the course is

One inherited engagement. The assistant is already running and already wrong in ways that look locally consistent. The student learns nine questions and uses them on a holdout the chapter does not walk through:

1. Whose clock is on the screen?
2. Whose decision is in the field?
3. Whose write did the tool accept?
4. Whose delivery actually happened?
5. Whose document was quoted?
6. Whose score was measured?
7. Whose signal should page a human?
8. Whose region actually ran, and which class of bytes was sent?
9. Can the same questions be applied when the nouns change?

A graduate can enter an unfamiliar operational mess, refuse a collapsed metric or a collapsed decision, put the idempotency key on the business operation, keep a forbidden document out of the score, and keep restricted bytes out of an account. They can say which managed services do not supply those guarantees.

## What the course is not

A tour of model vendors, a Kubernetes certificate, a clinical device, a copy of a national appointment system, or a claim that an exercise meets a statute. A graduate cannot, from this course alone, operate a hospital network or train a clinical model.

## How a lesson earns its place

A competent engineer would pause. The obvious fix passes a visible demo. A sealed holdout fails that fix. After the student writes a prediction, the chapter names the mechanism on a different object and, when the procedure is new, works one example. The graded row stays unnamed. The student then submits a binding. Software runs it on the practice log, the holdout, and a second mini-world with different nouns.

That sequence is the whole pedagogy. Motivational language is not a substitute for the holdout.
