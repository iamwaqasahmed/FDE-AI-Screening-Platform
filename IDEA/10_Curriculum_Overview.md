## Distinction, not doors

These do not open or close an investigation.

- Postgres for the outbox, same INV-04 tests.
- A real container image that matches the local promotion rules.
- Reciprocal rank fusion on a pair where each list alone fails, after INV-05.
- A linkage lab on a larger fixture, after the person rules in the gold are already respected by a deterministic partition the student can run as an optional file.
- A live account apply and destroy, after INV-08.
- One recording, if a reviewer exists, on an unseen log: name the relationship, refuse a repair that only renames a field.

## What was not given its own investigation

Friday calendars, person-versus-row splits, a rank-1 adapter, and a contact-center briefing are folded in. The calendar is a property inside INV-04’s file night. Person identity is a gold rule the partition extension may teach. The adapter is unnecessary once the model is not the writer of decisions. The handoff is the last page of INV-09: intent, what was tried, what is unresolved, and a next step a human may override. An invented clinical grade on that page fails INV-02’s contract.

A second industry is a later course. Clinic facts alone are too small. Nine unnamed puzzles are too large. This list is the size that survived both constraints.
