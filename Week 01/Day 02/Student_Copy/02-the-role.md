[Chapter 06](06-the-baselines.md) is the baselines. [Chapter 07](07-the-memo.md) is the memo, the brief, and the ranking. You do every row.

## Claim, request, and fact

You used these three kinds on Day 1. You will use them again in the brief.

A claim is their sentence. A figure on today's pages is a claim until a baseline prints the number, or until you can point at the number in a file and still call it their figure.

A request is what you will do next.

A fact is a sentence you can point at in a file already in this folder. The column name on the dashboard is a fact. The stage-gate line that a clinician assigns urgency is a fact. A leader's wish is not a fact.

The kickoff pages that still hold those rules are listed in [packet/kickoff/README.md](packet/kickoff/README.md). [Chapter 04](04-words.md) defines the words before you use them.
