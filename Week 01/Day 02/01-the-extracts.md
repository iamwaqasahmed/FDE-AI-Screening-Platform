The register names nine problems. The line next to each id is the symptom. It is not a measurement you have made.

On a page of your own, copy every leader figure you intend to use, and mark it as a claim. Name the file it came from. The brief you write later is that page.

The Day 1 interviews still stand. If a figure here contradicts a sentence you wrote down yesterday, add a row to that persona's notes. A new conversation uses the same shape as Day 1 and can live in `Week 01/Day 02/work/interviews`. The extracts are in the pack either way.

The price sheet is the cluster's unit prices. You will need it if you price a model call. You do not need it to finish the four baselines.
