## Block 1 — A claim beside a file

Open their worksheet of figures, then [chapter 02](02-the-extracts.md).

Ask which number they would refuse to treat as a measurement. Take the median of 14 days. It is a claim. The column `booking_date` is a fact, because it is named in [dashboard.md](sources/release/extracts/dashboard.md).

The usual miss is writing that waiting time fell.

## Block 2 — The two DNA lines

Open [chapter 03](03-the-baselines.md). Read `row 0.7692` and `person 0.0000`.

Ask which line they kept, and whether the same person can sit on both sides of the row split. The person line is the one in the memo. Show those two lines and stop there.

`appointment_status_final` repeats the label after the visit. The script leaves it unread.

## Block 3 — One approach row

Open [chapter 04](04-the-memo.md) beside their `work/approach.md`.

Read the `p-bounce` row aloud: `extract+retrieve`, because the specialty is a field and the return needs the rule that is in force.

Then read `p-triage`: only `blocked`. A second word on that row fails, and the check says `family does not fit` without naming `blocked`.

`p-slots` and `p-clock` are only `no-model`. The same message appears if a model word is added.

## Block 4 — The ranking

Open their `work/brief.md`. Any order of the nine ids can pass.

If `p-triage` is first, that line has to say that a clinician assigns urgency. [pol-stage-gate.md](sources/kickoff/corpus/pol-stage-gate.md) is the sentence.

When the check says `brief contains a banned sentence`, it does not name the sentence. A leader's wish is sitting outside a row whose Kind is `claim`. The usual ones today:

- `Waiting time fell last year.`
- `Al-Waha containment is about 70 percent.`
- `The assistant may set clinical urgency.`
- `No-shows are the largest cost in the cluster.`

Say those four once, and tell the room to keep them out of the ranking. The vendor percent belongs to a national pilot, in [vendor-note.md](sources/release/extracts/vendor-note.md).
