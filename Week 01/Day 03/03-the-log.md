# The log

The worker writes `scenario-log.json`. Do not hand-edit it. The check runs the worker again.

Copy [templates/intake.md](templates/intake.md) to `Week 01/Day 03/work/intake.md`.

The steps are the diagram. One step is the coordinator who holds the approval. The product manager writes the acceptance line: the workflow stops for that coordinator, and it does not book.

Under the in-flight heading, write that an in-flight referral keeps the criteria version it started with.

The architect owns the event contract in the release. The lead checks the SLA examples in the policy against the due dates in the log.
