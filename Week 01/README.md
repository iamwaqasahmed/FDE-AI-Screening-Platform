# Week 1

Arrival, then a choice of approach, then a workflow, then an agent, then a readout. You are not handed the assistant on the first morning. You earn the right to build a small proof, and you say what you will not build.

The four-week picture is on [the course guide](../learning/README.md). This page only places the five days.

| Day | Open | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Student copy](<Day 01/Student_Copy/README.md>) | Who asked, an access request, a pipeline that rejects a secret, two interviews |
| 2 | [Student copy](<Day 02/Student_Copy/README.md>) | Nine problems, four baselines, an approach memo |
| 3 | [Student copy](<Day 03/Student_Copy/README.md>) | `wf-intake` from `referral.submitted` to a held approval |
| 4 | [Student copy](<Day 04/Student_Copy/README.md>) | Status and booking for a patient. A return waits for a coordinator |
| 5 | [Student copy](<Day 05/Student_Copy/README.md>) | The score, the diagram, the design, the steering readout |

Start at Day 1 if you have not. Day 2 assumes the pipeline and the two interviews. Day 5 assumes the agent from Day 4.

When Day 5 is done, Week 2 begins with sign-in: [Week 2, Day 1](<../Week 02/Day 01/Student_Copy/README.md>).

Leave each day's `Solution_Copy` closed until that day's finish chapter says the day is done.
