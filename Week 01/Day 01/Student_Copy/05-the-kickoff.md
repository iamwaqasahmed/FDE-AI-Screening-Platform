# 05 — The kickoff

The kickoff is what the Chief Digital and Transformation Officer's office would hand you on the morning of 10 January 2027. Read the five files in the order below, and write on the worksheets as you go. The list in [packet/kickoff](packet/kickoff/README.md) is only the drawer. This chapter is the order.

The worksheets stay in `notes/`. The scan reads `work/`, so leave the worksheets out of `work/`.

## Do this

1. Open [packet/kickoff/world/zones.md](packet/kickoff/world/zones.md).

   A zone is a place a system can run. A facility line names the zone that facility sits in. On [notes/zones.md](notes/zones.md), place `h-nakhl`, `h-safra`, `h-wadi`, and `h-ain`. [Chapter 03](03-the-client.md) named three places. It does not place the hospitals. Copy the zone written on the same line as each id. Ain is on this page. If a primary care center has no zone on that page, leave it off the sketch. Your sketch uses this file and nothing later. You draw it yourself.

2. Open [packet/kickoff/corpus/pol-stage-gate.md](packet/kickoff/corpus/pol-stage-gate.md).

   The lines at the top, between the `---` marks, are the document's label. `status: in_force` means this page is the rule today. A gate is an approval, not a meeting. The page then lists G0 through G5 in order.

   On [notes/my-page.md](notes/my-page.md), write:

   - who the sponsor is
   - who would use a front desk
   - which gate you are in today
   - one thing that document requires before anyone calls the work a pilot

   This file does not name the sponsor or the users. The sponsor is Layla Al-Harbi, the person whose sentence you copied in [chapter 03](03-the-client.md). The users are people. Name these three groups: patients who need a booking, a reschedule, an answer, or a referral chased; the contact-center staff and the coordinators who do that work now; and the primary-care clinicians who send referrals. Referrals, appointments, reminders, and the contact center are the work those people do. They are not the users.

   Match your day to the list. You can name a problem, the users, and a sponsor. You do not have a risk class, a personal-data assessment (the file calls that a DPIA), or a pilot approval. The gate in front of you is the list item that asks only for the problem, the users, and the sponsor. Write that item's code, G0 or whichever code the file prints on that line.

   The pilot is the list item that says a limited pilot is allowed. Copy what that item requires, including the sentence about who assigns clinical urgency.

3. Open [packet/kickoff/corpus/pol-assistant-use.md](packet/kickoff/corpus/pol-assistant-use.md).

   On the same worksheet, write what stays out of a prompt and out of `work/`. The page's first rule is that coding assistants may be used. The rest of the page is the list that stays out. Write both. The page does not name the three kinds of material a prompt may hold. [Chapter 07](07-the-pipeline.md) names those three. The knowledge answer needs that list as well as the prohibitions. The same text is already inside the starter as `policy/pol-assistant-use.md`. Leave that file as the cluster wrote it.

4. Open [packet/kickoff/corpus/doc-postmortem-2023.md](packet/kickoff/corpus/doc-postmortem-2023.md), then [packet/kickoff/corpus/doc-minutes-2023-11.md](packet/kickoff/corpus/doc-minutes-2023-11.md).

   The postmortem is the write-up. The minutes are the room. On [notes/reading-notes.md](notes/reading-notes.md), write what each document says in its own row before you decide what the 2023 chatbot teaches. If the two rows do not say the same thing, keep both. Do not let the write-up replace the minutes. Then write what you are willing to treat as settled, and what you still need to ask someone.
