# 02 — The role

[Chapter 00](00-background.md) said what the four weeks produce. This chapter is how you behave on the client's site today. Nothing is filed yet.

A forward deployed engineer works on the client's problem, inside the client's constraints, and leaves something the client can run and check. On this engagement the client is the Al-Waha Health Cluster. You are with the patient-access service.

## The loop for today

1. Hear the ask.
2. Separate a claim from something you can point at in a file you already hold.
3. Ask for the access you need today.
4. Leave a small pipeline a check can run.
5. Talk to two people who live with the work.
6. Stop at the gate you are actually in.

A claim is their sentence. A request is what you will do next. A fact is a sentence you can point at in a file already in the [kickoff](packet/kickoff/README.md). You will use these three kinds in the interviews. [Chapter 08](08-the-interviews.md) shows a short example before you write the real notes.

## The lifecycle you will use

The cluster moves an AI system through stage gates. A gate is an approval. The document that names the gates is in the kickoff, and you read it in [chapter 05](05-the-kickoff.md). Today you learn which gate is in front of you. You do not borrow a lifecycle from another company.
