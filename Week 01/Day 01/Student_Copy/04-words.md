# 04 — Words

Read this page once. You will look back at it while you fill the form and the notes. You do not need to memorize it.

These are the cluster's words for today. A definition here is the everyday meaning. When you need the rule, open the document the kickoff already contains.

## The service

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Health cluster | A group of hospitals and primary care centers that plan and deliver care for one population |
| Primary health care center (PHC) | The local clinic that refers a patient onward |
| Referral | A request from a PHC, or another clinic, for a hospital appointment |
| Return (bounce) | The hospital sends the referral back because it cannot accept it as written |
| Coordinator | The staff member who accepts, returns, or redirects referrals |
| Contact center | The cluster's phone and chat service |
| E-referral | The platform that carries referrals from primary care centers to hospitals |
| Working day | Sunday to Thursday. Friday and Saturday are the weekend |
| Ramadan hours | The clinic times the cluster publishes for Ramadan. They differ from the rest of the year |

## Care

These words appear in today's briefing, on the persona cards, and in the sample referral letter.

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Outpatient | A clinic visit where the patient goes home the same day. Not an overnight hospital stay |
| Specialty | One hospital service line. Endocrinology is hormones and diabetes. Ophthalmology is eyes |
| Diabetes mellitus (DM) | Diabetes. In a referral note, "DM fu" means a diabetes follow-up |
| General hospital | A hospital for a wide range of care. Nakhl and Safra are general hospitals |
| Specialist hospital | A hospital focused on particular specialties. Wadi is the specialist hospital |
| Diabetes and eye center | Ain. Diabetes care and eye care on one site |
| Triage | Sorting how urgent a problem is. A clinician does this. The assistant does not |
| Clinician | A doctor or other professional who decides on care |
| Clinical urgency | How serious a problem is, and how soon it needs a person. A clinician assigns it |
| Appointment | A booked time for a patient to be seen |
| Slot | One bookable appointment on a clinic's schedule |
| Did not attend (DNA) | The patient had a booking and did not come. Staff also call this a no-show |
| Reminder | A message sent to the patient before an appointment |
| Queue | The line of referrals waiting for a person to accept, return, or redirect them |

## Data and decisions

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Protected health information (PHI) | Information that identifies a patient in the context of care. The cluster decides which fields count |
| Classification | The label on an access request. The labels you may write today are below |
| In force | A document whose status, dates, and site make it the rule today. A draft is not in force |
| Stage gate | A required approval point on the path from idea to go-live |
| Human approval | A person accepts or changes an assistant suggestion before it takes effect |
| Red-flag phrase | Wording the assistant must hand to a human. The assistant does not grade urgency |

## Classification labels

Write one of these on the access request.

| Label | Use it when |
| --- | --- |
| `public` | The cluster has already classed the content as public. Clinic hours and public leaflets are this kind of material |
| `internal` | The material is for staff. It is not a patient record, and it is not for the public. Today's kickoff and the dev credentials are internal |
| `phi` | The request is for information that identifies a patient in the context of care. Name, phone, date of birth, and a national id are this kind of field |

## Places

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Private cloud | The cluster's own cloud, on two sites, where PHI may be processed |
| External model | The contracted public-cloud language model, for public content |
| Zone | The name the kickoff uses for one of the three places. You copy the names from the zones file |
