## What must be true to pass gate G0?

Gate G0 is intake, from [pol-stage-gate.md](sources/kickoff/corpus/pol-stage-gate.md). To pass it, the problem, the users, and the sponsor are named. Naming them is not the same as accepting the sponsor's sentence. The gate is an approval, and it is the gate in front of us today.

The users and the pilot gate are written out in [chapter 02](02-the-reading-and-the-request.md).

[pol-assistant-use.md](sources/kickoff/corpus/pol-assistant-use.md) says a coding assistant may be used, and it lists what stays out: patient data, secrets, unpublished cluster documents, the sample referral letter, and the watermark in the dev credentials.
