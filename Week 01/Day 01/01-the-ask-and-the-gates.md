The Chief Digital and Transformation Officer wants a virtual front desk live before Ramadan, including triage. That sentence is in the [case](../../case/README.md). Gate G0 asks you to name the problem, the users, and the sponsor. It does not ask you to accept the sentence.

[pol-stage-gate.md](../../data/rel-w1d1/corpus/pol-stage-gate.md) is in force. A draft is not. The six gates are G0 through G5. A clinician assigns every clinical urgency. The assistant routes emergency wording to a human.

On a page of your own, write:

- Who the sponsor is.
- Who would use a front desk.
- Which gate you are in today.
- One thing the stage-gate document requires before anyone calls the work a pilot.

The architect sketches the three zones from [zones.md](../../data/rel-w1d1/world/zones.md) and nothing later. Name `zone-hospital`, `zone-private`, and `zone-public`, and which of `h-nakhl`, `h-safra`, `h-wadi`, and `h-ain` sits in which zone.

The postmortem is the write-up. The minutes are the room. Read both before you decide what the 2023 chatbot teaches.

Copy [forms/access-request.md](../../data/rel-w1d1/forms/access-request.md) to `Week 01/Day 01/work/access-request.md` and fill every field. Name the requester, the data you need today, the purpose, and a classification. Today you need the kickoff files and [credentials/dev.json](../../data/rel-w1d1/credentials/dev.json). You do not need the full history. A blank field is not a request.
