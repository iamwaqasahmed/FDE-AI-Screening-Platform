# 02 — The role

[Chapter 00](00-background.md) said what the four weeks produce. This chapter is how you behave on the client's site today. Nothing is filed yet.

A forward deployed engineer works on the client's problem, inside the client's constraints, and leaves something the client can run and check. On this engagement the client is the Al-Waha Health Cluster. You are with the patient-access service. You hold all five parts yourself.

## The five parts today

| Part | What you will leave |
| --- | --- |
| Lead | The sprint, with the deploy before the readout |
| AI or data engineer | A health probe that reports the registered hash |
| Application engineer | The image, the manifest, the router, the credentials, the four tests, and the work pipeline |
| Product manager | The operator steps for the health probe |
| Architect | The URL mapped onto `zone-private`, and the deployment note |

You do these in [chapter 05](05-the-sprint.md), [chapter 06](06-the-image.md), [chapter 07](07-the-probe.md), [chapter 08](08-the-endpoint.md), and [chapter 09](09-the-pages.md), in that order. [Chapter 10](10-finish.md) files the two answers and runs the check.

There is no interview today. The check does not read an interview note. It does not read a provenance note, a limits note, a rollback note, or a quota note. [Omar Al-Zahrani](packet/kickoff/personas/ciso.md) is the Chief Information Security Officer. His id is `ciso`. Today's drop is from him. You read the drop. You do not interview him on this page.
