# 02 — The role

[Chapter 00](00-background.md) said what the four weeks produce. This chapter is how you behave on the client's site today. Nothing is filed yet.

A forward deployed engineer works on the client's problem, inside the client's constraints, and leaves something the client can run and check. On this engagement the client is the Al-Waha Health Cluster. You are with the patient-access service. You hold all five parts yourself.

## The five parts today

| Part | What you will leave |
| --- | --- |
| Lead | The sprint: smoke, then the change, then the readout, then the retro |
| AI or data engineer | The model limit: `local-small`, the quota, and what Stage 2 adds |
| Application engineer | The smoke files left in place, and the booking, the file, the notice, and the contract replaced |
| Product manager | What a patient at Ain sees: a queued booking |
| Architect | The nightly file path in `zone-hospital`, and the deployment note |

You do these in [chapter 05](05-the-sprint.md), [chapter 06](06-the-booking.md), [chapter 07](07-the-contract.md), [chapter 08](08-the-pages.md), and [chapter 09](09-the-readout.md), in that order. [Chapter 10](10-finish.md) files the two answers and runs the four checks.
