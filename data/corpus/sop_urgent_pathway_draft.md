# Urgent pathway

Status: NOT ISSUED. Draft for clinical review only.

This note is not current policy. The issued screening procedure remains in force until the clinical lead and the data protection officer both sign this draft.

## Proposed rule

When a grader records sight-threatening disease, the person should be seen by ophthalmology within 14 days of the result.

Mild referable disease should stay on a community recall. It should not enter the ophthalmology queue.

## What would have to be true before this draft is used

- The issued procedure is updated.
- The result letter can name an urgent appointment separately from a routine referral.
- The clinical lead is the named safety owner.
