# Data-protection operating rule

Status: In force.

Retinal photos and screening records are health data. A service that handles them must follow this rule.

## Boundaries

- Images and extracts stay on the authority network.
- Every access is written to an access log.
- Patient images are not used to train a model unless a separate approval is signed. A software pilot does not include that approval.
- A generated letter must not contain a diagnosis. It may repeat a code a grader has already recorded.

## Care-changing results

A grader signs every result that refers, recalls, or reassures a patient. A model score may sort a queue or flag a photo to retake. A model score must not discharge a patient or create a referral.

## Review size

The data protection officer will review a bounded service with a data map. The officer will not review a platform that covers breast, cervical, and eye screening in one release.
