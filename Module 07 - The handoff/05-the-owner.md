# The owner

[handoff/owner.md](../handoff/owner.md) names the operational contact and the clinical safety owner.

The operational contact is the Director of Screening Programs. That person can be called about the runbook.

`clinical_safety_owner` is `unassigned`. `pilot_starts` is `no`. The ophthalmology lead has not signed. Until that line is a person's name, the pilot does not start. Do not leave the field blank and also write that the pilot has started.
