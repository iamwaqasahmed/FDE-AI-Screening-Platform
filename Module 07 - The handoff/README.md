# Module 07 — The handoff

Leave the service with the two people who have to live with it. The Chief Digital Officer wants a quarter they can show the ministry. The ophthalmology lead wants a shorter list and a signature on anything that changes care.

You write one page for the executive, one page for the clinical lead, and a runbook: how to start, how to read a failure, who to call, and what the service will not do. Both pages describe the same scope. A named owner on the authority side is part of the pack. If that person is still unknown, the pilot does not start.

This module is ready when someone who was not in the build can operate the service for a week from the runbook.
