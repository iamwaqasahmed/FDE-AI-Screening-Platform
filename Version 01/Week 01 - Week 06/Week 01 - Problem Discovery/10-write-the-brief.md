# Write the brief

**Day 5.** Write this before you open the [debrief](12-debrief.md). Two pages is enough. Use [the brief template](templates/brief.md). Save it as `brief.md` in the work folder.

The checker looks for key facts, not for a heading style. Name dates as `YYYY-MM-DD`. A count may use a thousands separator.

1. **Ask, verbatim.** The board paper's request, still intact.
2. **Client and sponsor.** Who can say yes, and the two dates: the one the board was told, and the one they are now asking for.
3. **Who feels the pain.** One sentence each for the grader, the ophthalmology lead, the patient, and the data protection officer.
4. **Workflow as it is.** Register, invite, photograph, grade, letter, specialist. Mark the handoffs that break.
5. **Job to be done.** The request was to make the vendor model live. The job is the outcome the programme cannot currently produce.
6. **Outcome and metrics.** One primary outcome. The counts you will stand behind: eligible people, the letter-delay window, the two backlog figures and why each is wrong.
7. **Constraints.** The blank clinical safety owner, so the pilot stays stopped. The vendor scope is primary-care. The registry freeze date. Deceased rows are not eligible.
8. **First slice.** What you will do first, and the decision a human still owns. Identity resolution and the letter fix unlock the rest. Say that in those words.
9. **Later work.** The vendor expansion does not come first. The workshop's top idea is not the business case. The reminder's before-and-after is not the effect: there was no control group, and seasonality moves the rate.
10. **Open questions.** What would change the slice. Leave the true open backlog and the true reminder effect open if you could not measure them. That is honest.
11. **Two conversations.** Five lines for the Chief Digital Officer. Five lines for the ophthalmology lead. Same slice.

Do not write that the vendor model expansion should come first, that the reminder caused the whole rise in attendance, or that deceased people are eligible. Those sentences fail the brief on their own.

**Finish.** `brief.md` is about two pages, and `findings.json` is complete.
