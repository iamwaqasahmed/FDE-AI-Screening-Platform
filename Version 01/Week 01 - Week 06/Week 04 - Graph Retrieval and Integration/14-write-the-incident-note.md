# Write the incident note

**Day 5. Be the consultant, then the product manager.** Write this before you open the [debrief](16-debrief.md). Two pages is enough. Use [the incident-note template](templates/incident-note.md). Save it as `incident-note.md`.

The checker looks for key facts, not for a heading style. Name dates as `YYYY-MM-DD`. A count may use a thousands separator. Quote the cost in whole pounds.

1. **What happened.** The window, as two dates. The notice's two claims do not survive the files. Do not write the notice's sentence as your own.
2. **What the files show.** How many requests were lost. How many dispatch rows are second copies.
3. **Who was not reached.** The local ids inside the upper bound, and the people. Say that the figure is an upper bound. The chase list is not the overdue cohort.
4. **What not to send.** People already seen, and people flagged deceased. A date of birth does not cross to the bureau.
5. **The precedent.** INC-0233. The 2025 file had no request id.
6. **The re-send.** How many letters you would print again. The next letters release window. A print run is not a release. The chase template is the programme manager's decision. Do not invent the wording.
7. **Cost.** The total, in whole pounds.
8. **Decisions a person owns.** The data protection officer has not decided whether a chase file may sit with the bureau. The safety owner is unnamed. The pilot stays stopped. Do not invent either decision.
9. **Two conversations.** Five lines for the programme manager. Five lines for the bureau. The same counts. To the programme manager: who was not reached, and who must not be written to. To the bureau: the notice is wrong, and the files have to be kept until the retention date.

Do not write that no letters were lost, that the dispatch file is complete, that the chase list is the overdue cohort, that the pilot may start, that dates of birth may leave the authority zone, that every lost letter should be re-sent, or that M06 may read the lost-letter names. Those sentences fail the note on their own.

**Finish.** `incident-note.md` is about two pages, and `findings.json` is complete.
