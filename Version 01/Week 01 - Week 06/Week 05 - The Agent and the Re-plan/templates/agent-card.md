# Agent card

## 1. Loss table

Ten baseline failures, and what each one lacked: a second step, a stop that should have fired, a stop that should not have fired, a pause, or the path.

## 2. Baseline rates

Single-loop overall percent. Keyword-veto overall percent.

## 3. Tier

Tier 2. What would make it tier 3, and what tier 3 lacks.

## 4. Tools

What the agent calls, and the week that built each one.

## 5. State

What a checkpoint holds, and what it does not hold.

## 6. Veto

The eight families and the document each one cites. Where the check sits.

## 7. Approvals

Who pauses the line, the re-send, the risk, and the roadmap change.

## 8. Account and models

The client, that it is read-only, and which models may see a local id. The generator that does not fit a multi-hop step.

## 9. The log

References, not values. Where a patient-record read goes.

## 10. The veto sample

Stop tasks, families, and the Wilson lower bound.

## 11. What moved a slice

Each change you kept, and the slice it moved. A change that moved nothing is not listed.

## 12. Not covered

Deploying the agent. Any framework you tried and the slice it did not move.
