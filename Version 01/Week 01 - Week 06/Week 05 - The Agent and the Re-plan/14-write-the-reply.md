# Write the reply

**Day 5. Be the consultant, then the product manager.** Write this before you open the [debrief](16-debrief.md). One page is enough. Use [the reply template](templates/cdo-reply.md). Save it as `cdo-reply.md`.

The checker looks for key facts, not for a heading style. Name dates as `YYYY-MM-DD`. A count may use a thousands separator. The contract value and the corrected cashable figure are whole pounds.

1. **The answer.** One line. The requested date does not work. Give the earliest go-live and the latest, and say that procurement binds.
2. **The site.** Name it. It is a hospital clinic. Say that it needs a data protection decision. Do not say it joins.
3. **The route and the gate.** The contract value, a framework call-off, and the Gate 2 date.
4. **The backlog.** The build date, and that it does not bind. One sentence.
5. **The business case.** The corrected cashable figure. The safety owner is unnamed.
6. **The risk and the readiness gate.** R-07, and the date readiness is not before.
7. **The stop.** The pilot stays stopped. Do not say it may start.
8. **Next week.** The conditions a clinical safety owner would set are not in this drop. Say that the decision is not this week's.
9. **The line.** It is with the Chief Digital Officer for approval. It is not published.

Do not write that 2027-02-01 is achievable, that the second site joins the pilot, or that the pilot may start. Those sentences fail the reply on their own.

**Finish.** `cdo-reply.md` is about a page, and `findings.json` is complete.
