# Write the data note

**Day 5. Be the consultant, then the product manager.** Write this before you open the [debrief](14-debrief.md). Two pages is enough. Use [the data-note template](templates/data-note.md). Save it as `data-note.md`.

The checker looks for key facts, not for a heading style. Name dates as `YYYY-MM-DD`. A count may use a thousands separator.

1. **What changed.** `HOME_CLINIC` became `REGISTERED_SITE`. A batch of registry numbers was reissued. Give the count.
2. **The counts you will stand behind.** Eligible people. Coverage under the two-year rule. The blank seen-date count, named as an upper bound and not as a number of people.
3. **What the contracts guarantee.** A missing column fails. A silent drop is a failure. Identity resolution is the input the other cohorts rerun on.
4. **The freeze.** The date it starts, and the registry release date after it.
5. **Decisions a person owns.** The link threshold. Sign-off for the eligibility rule. The owner of the person record, who is not in the RACI. Do not invent the owner.
6. **Two conversations.** Five lines for the Chief Digital Officer. Five lines for the records manager. Same counts.

The pilot stays stopped until a clinical safety owner is named. Say that.

Do not write that each registry number belongs to one person, that the same name and date of birth are the same person, that a blank seen date means the patient was not seen, that deceased people are eligible, or that the MRN identifies one person. Those sentences fail the note on their own.

**Finish.** `data-note.md` is about two pages, and `findings.json` is complete.
