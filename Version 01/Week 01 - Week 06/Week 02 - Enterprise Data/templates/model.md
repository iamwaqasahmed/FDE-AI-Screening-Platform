# Warehouse model

- **Person.** One row per cluster.
- **Identifier bridge.** System, value, valid from, valid to.
- **Registry snapshots.** Schema version on each load.
- **Encounters and referrals.** Where the seen date came from.
- **Rules.** Document id, days, and the clause they replace in `build.sql`.
- **Zone.** Where a date of birth may be used to link.
