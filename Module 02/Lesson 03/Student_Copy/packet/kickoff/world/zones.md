# Where the cluster runs

The engagement opens on 2027-01-10. Working days are Sunday, Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday. Friday and Saturday are the weekend.

## Zones

- `zone-hospital`: Hospital data centers
- `zone-private`: Private cloud (site-a, site-b)
- `zone-public`: Public cloud (ext-large)

## Facilities

- `h-nakhl`: Nakhl General Hospital, in `zone-hospital`
- `h-safra`: Safra General Hospital, in `zone-hospital`
- `h-wadi`: Wadi Specialist Hospital, in `zone-private`
- `h-ain`: Ain Diabetes and Eye Center, in `zone-hospital`
- `phc-nakhl-01`: Nakhl Central PHC, a primary health care center
- `phc-safra-03`: Safra South PHC, a primary health care center
- `phc-wadi-02`: Wadi Family PHC, a primary health care center
- `phc-rim`: Rim Village PHC, a primary health care center
