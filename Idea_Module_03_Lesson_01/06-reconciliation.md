# 06 — Reconciliation with the data thread

`Idea_CrossCutting/05-data-and-evaluation/01-data-thread.md` says Splink is the linker to teach. This lesson’s dossier says the finish check should stay the deterministic partition, and Splink should be a sketch on the pairs those rules do not decide.

Both are right about a different file.

The checker in `problem_simulation/alwaha/check/identity_m03_l01.py` grades `partition()` as an exact group match. `synthetic_m03_l01.py` grades a hand-built sample against the fidelity card, the watermark, and national ids. It does not train on synthetic rows and test on real letters. `problem_simulation/design/11-eval-and-hardness.md` still describes identity as precision and recall against point-in-time gold, and the synthetic check as train-on-synthetic, test-on-real. Those two sentences overclaim what the code does. Fix the table, or build the tests. Do not leave the table as the description of this release.

Fellegi–Sunter weights need enough leftover pairs to estimate m and u, and they add across columns only if the comparisons are independent. A shared national id is the mother-and-newborn failure on this waitlist and legitimate evidence for an Iqama `replaces` link. Expectation maximisation on the lesson’s short gold file will not produce a threshold worth grading. Splink’s own `fake_1000` demo and its 0.95 cutoff are demo settings, not a clinical rule.

**Resolution.** This lesson grades the vetoes: mother and newborn stay apart, twins stay apart, a reused MRN stays apart, an Iqama replacement is followed, Hijri dates follow the tabular function already in `dates.py`, labels follow `ann-v2`, and the canary scan is a leak detector. The student writes a short note that names the independence assumption and says which pairs the rules refuse to score. Installing Splink, regenerating gold with hijridate, or choosing an epsilon are not finish checks.

A later optional lab can grade Splink on a larger fixture, after dates are normalized to one civil calendar, with a clerical band for middle scores and a held-out set of links. That lab is where “Splink is the method” becomes hands-on. It is not this release.

A fidelity card and a clean canary scan are not differential privacy and not anonymisation. NIST SP 800-226 is the definition to name. This lesson sets no epsilon. The PDPL notes stay orientation from the English PDF. The case law stays fictional.
