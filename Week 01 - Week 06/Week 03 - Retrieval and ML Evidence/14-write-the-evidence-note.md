# Write the evidence note

**Day 5. Be the consultant, then the product manager.** Write this before you open the [debrief](16-debrief.md). Two pages is enough. Use [the evidence-note template](templates/evidence-note.md). Save it as `evidence-note.md`.

The checker looks for key facts, not for a heading style. Name dates as `YYYY-MM-DD`. A count may use a thousands separator. Quote an AUC to two decimal places.

1. **What changed.** The effective date. The sites that remain, and the site that was removed.
2. **Which outputs changed.** The three contracts. The vendor cohort and its split. The clusters, which did not change.
3. **The index.** Answers cite a document in force, and they cite the original rather than a forward.
4. **The vendor on its cohort.** The captures that remain, how many of them were adjudicated, and the sensitivity against that label. Do not call the routine grade the ground truth.
5. **The claims.** The figure 0.95, and that it does not hold on the adjudicated cohort. ClearSight was stopped. The post-mortem blames accuracy. The pilot stays stopped until a safety owner is named.
6. **The did-not-attend number.** The leaky column. The random-split AUC and the honest AUC, each to two decimal places.
7. **Decisions a person owns.** The EU region in the vendor agreement. Whether the file name crosses. `DOB` stays inside. `YEAR_OF_BIRTH` is the replacement if a year is required. The safety owner is unnamed. Do not invent the owner, and do not invent the data protection officer's decision on the region.
8. **Two conversations.** Five lines for the Chief Digital Officer. Five lines for the data protection officer. The same counts. The overdue figure in the pilot scope is an upper bound. Give the number.

Do not write that the claimed sensitivity holds for every patient, that the routine grade is the ground truth, that a random split is an honest split, that the pilot may start, that dates of birth may leave the authority zone, that ClearSight was deployed successfully, or that M06 may read patient letters. Those sentences fail the note on their own.

**Finish.** `evidence-note.md` is about two pages, and `findings.json` is complete.
