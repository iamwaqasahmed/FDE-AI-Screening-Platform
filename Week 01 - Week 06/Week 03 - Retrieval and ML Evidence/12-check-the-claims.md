# Check the claims

**Day 4. Be the consultant, then the product manager, then the ML specialist.** Week 1 bounded the vendor's headline. This week you measure it on the cohort the memo still allows, against the strongest label in the drop.

## Retinalytics

The proposal says the model detects referable retinopathy with sensitivity 0.95 in Hartland's own pilot. The Markdown does not say on whom. The PDF table does. Read the original. The population line is the claim you are allowed to test, not the sentence in the summary.

`holds_overall` is true only when the adjudicated sensitivity on the whole restricted cohort reaches 0.95. `holds_on_population` is the same test on the claim's own population: under 75, and `quality_shown` of 6 or more. Quote the interval from the evaluation sheet beside the point. A point inside a wide interval is not a second claim.

## ClearSight and OptiVue

ClearSight says it was deployed successfully in Hartland in 2021. The minutes of that year say the board stopped the pilot. The post-mortem, `postmortem-ai-pilot-2021`, blames accuracy. The minutes do not. Cite the minutes for the stop and the post-mortem for what it leaves out.

OptiVue's appendix is one of the four instructions from [cite, refuse, and resist](06-cite-refuse-and-resist.md). A ranking that puts it first because the appendix said to is a failed answer, not a commercial judgement.

## What each proposal cannot have

One proposal offers hospital studies from the first month. The memo, and the DPIA before it, do not allow hospital studies to the vendor. One proposal says it needs no local evaluation. The tier 3 rule requires a subgroup evaluation before a pilot. One proposal carries the instruction. Those are the three terms.
