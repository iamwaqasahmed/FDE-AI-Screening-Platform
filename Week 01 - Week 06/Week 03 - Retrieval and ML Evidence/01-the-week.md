# The week

**Day 1. Everyone.**

Week 2 ended with one cluster per person and a vendor cohort counted in captures. This morning the data protection officer restricted that pathway. "Today" is 19 October 2026. The registry freeze that started this morning is not your problem. Nothing you ship this week needs a registry release.

Your job is not to start the pilot. It is to answer the corpus with a citation a records manager can check, to measure the vendor on the captures that may still leave the authority, and to tell the data protection officer which outputs changed.

Nothing here is a real patient's record. Do not grade a photograph, and do not give clinical advice.

## The team

The same five or six people. `engagement/workstreams/contracts.csv` still makes you integrate. C-01 still says the clusters key the vendor cohort. C-03, C-05, and C-08 are the three this drop changes.

| Role | The hard question this week |
|---|---|
| Data engineer | An index that keeps status, effective date, sunset, and site on every chunk, and that reads the original when the Markdown does not |
| Data scientist | The pathway question answered from the document in force, and the did-not-attend number after the leak is removed |
| ML specialist | The vendor on the restricted cohort, against adjudication, by subgroup. The headline is a subgroup |
| Software engineer | Answers that cite, refuse, and do not follow an instruction written inside a document. The budget is in pounds |
| Architect | Which approved model may see which text, and a contract that fails before a date of birth leaves the authority |
| Product manager | The impact log. Three contracts rerun. The clusters do not |
| Consultant | The evidence note. Two readers. The claim does not survive, and the pilot stays stopped |

If the team is smaller, pair the architect with the software engineer and the product manager with the consultant. Do not drop a question.

## Out of scope

Graph retrieval, and any question that needs more than one document joined by a link. That is Week 4. An agent that plans its own steps. That is Week 5. An LLM judge: `eval/judge_calibration.jsonl` quotes the answer key, so leave the file closed. Retraining the vendor, or moving its threshold. Autonomous grading, any referral a grader has not signed, and image bytes. The pilot stays stopped.

A technique earns its place only on a slice where a simpler one lost. A dense embedding is optional. It has to move a slice the baselines lost, and the card has to say which slice.
