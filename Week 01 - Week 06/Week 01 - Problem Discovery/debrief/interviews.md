# Debrief — interviews

A good script opens a topic and then asks the follow-up. The opening line is what a weak script gets twelve times. These are the facts the practice seed was withholding. Another seed changes the numbers and the dates. The topics do not change.

## Two disagreements

Since `2025-10-01` the ophthalmology lead's list shows **3,826** referrals with no seen date. The messages file matches that number, because booked and seen were blanked together. The board pack's **941** counts only fast-track booking breaches. Neither is the open count. The hidden open count, visits that never happened and were not did-not-attends, is **403**. The clinic letters hold some of the missing seen dates and they are a sample, so this drop cannot produce 403 by itself. A count below 3,826 that is not 941 is the honest week-1 answer. Quoting either person as the truth fails.

The programme manager says the 2023 letter delays were a grading problem. They were the bureau migration, `CHG-0158`.
