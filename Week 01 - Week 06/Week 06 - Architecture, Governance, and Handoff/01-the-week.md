# The week

**Day 1. You.**

Week 5 said no to the date and the site, and it stopped the agent when a rule forbade the action. This morning a clinical director writes that she will be the clinical safety owner, with conditions. "Today" is 9 November 2026. The handoff is Friday. The owners register has not moved.

Your job is not to start the pilot. It is to say which conditions are met, to name the person who has to act on each one, to record a design the rules will allow, and to hand over pages a reader can use.

Nothing here is a real patient's record. Do not grade a photograph, and do not give clinical advice.

## The hats

You wear every hat below. `engagement/workstreams/contracts.csv` sets the order of your outputs. No row names week 6. C-05 is the one you deliver in full: the architecture and the data flows, for the security review. C-06, C-07, and C-08 are consumed: the date, the people who were not reached, and which captures may cross. C-01 to C-04 do not change.

| Hat | The hard question this week |

| Be the consultant | A handoff that names the owner in the memo and still says the pilot is stopped |

Do not drop a question.

## Out of scope

Editing `owners.csv`, the RACI, the risk register, the issues log, or the roadmap. Signing the hazard log. Publishing the transparency record. Deploying the service. Contacting a patient, the press, the minister, or the vendor. Sending or printing a letter. A write token. The vendor model, photographs, and clinical judgement. An LLM judge: `data/eval` stays closed. The pilot stays stopped.
