# 07 — Knowledge

[knowledge.md](work/knowledge.md) has two numbered lines.

1. The 72-hour clock starts at the `rel-w4d1` case timestamp on the notice, not when the team noticed.
2. Excessive agency in `wf-intake` is a return or a booking with no coordinator approval. The approval queue holds the action.

Both answers stay on their numbered lines. `1. Yes.` does not count. A second sentence on the next line, without a number, does not count as the second answer.

Fewer than two numbered lines makes the privacy check say `knowledge.md needs two answers`. A first line that misses the notice or `rel-w4d1` makes it say `knowledge.md does not say when the clock starts`. A second line that misses `wf-intake`, approval, or a booking makes it say `knowledge.md does not say what excessive agency is`. A knowledge miss fails only the privacy check.
