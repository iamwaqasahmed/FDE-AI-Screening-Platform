# 06 — Knowledge

[knowledge.md](work/knowledge.md) has two numbered lines.

1. The three drifts are ramadan hours, criteria v4, and the campaign clinic.
2. document.changed invalidates a cached answer.

Both answers stay on their numbered lines. `1. Yes.` does not count. A second sentence on the next line, without a number, does not count as the second answer.

Fewer than two numbered lines makes the incident check say `knowledge.md needs two answers`. A first line that misses one of the three names makes it say `knowledge.md does not name the three drifts`. A second line that misses `document.changed` makes it say `knowledge.md does not say which event drops the cache`. A knowledge miss fails only the incident check.

The trainer's session is [chapter 07](07-trainer.md). Students can skip it.
