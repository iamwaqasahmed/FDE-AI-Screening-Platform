# 06 — Knowledge

[knowledge.md](work/knowledge.md) has two numbered lines.

1. A new consultant leave creates session.cancelled.
2. p-clock is first. p-waitlist depends on identity.

Both answers stay on their numbered lines. The first numbered line contains `session.cancelled`. The second names a first problem that is not `p-triage` and not `vendor-bot`, and it names a dependency. `1. Yes.` does not count. A second sentence on the next line, without a number, does not count as the second answer.

A first line that misses `session.cancelled` makes the readout check say `knowledge.md does not say which event leave creates`. That line is the leave-event line. It is not `leave does not emit session.cancelled`. A second line that misses a first problem or a dependency makes the readout check say `knowledge.md does not name a first problem and a dependency`. A knowledge miss fails `chk-readout`.

The trainer's session is [chapter 07](07-trainer.md). Students can skip it.
