# 02 — The sprint

[sprint.md](work/sprint.md) opens the requests, then sign-in, then the bus, then the handoff, then leave, then the warehouse, then the ranking, then the proposal. The application engineer owns the handoff. The product manager owns the ranking and the roadmap. The architect owns both architecture documents. The AI engineer brings measured results into the success metrics. The lead presents.

The shell page has an empty Order section. An empty page, or a page that names a later word first, makes the integrate check say `sprint.md does not open the requests first`. A sprint miss fails `chk-w4-integrate`.

[README.md](sources/release/README.md) says the contact center, the warehouse feed, and HR asked to be connected. [requests.json](sources/release/connect/requests.json) names cc, bi, and hr. Staff sign-in and the bus are the two confirmations.
