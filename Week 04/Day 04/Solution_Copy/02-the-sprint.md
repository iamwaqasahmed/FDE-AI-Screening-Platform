# 02 — The sprint

[sprint.md](work/sprint.md) opens the drill, then the replicas, then the failover, then the breaker, then the patient view, then the trade-off. The application engineer runs the drill. The AI engineer confirms the hash on site-b. The product manager writes the patient view. The architect owns rent versus buy. The lead checks the Ramadan date.

The shell page has an empty Order section. An empty page, or a page that names a later word first, makes the slo check say `sprint.md does not open the drill first`. A sprint miss fails `chk-w4-slo`.

[README.md](sources/release/README.md) says the site-a failover drill is open and site-b is the second private-cloud site. Do not copy the date. [site-a.json](sources/release/drill/site-a.json) names the killed site. Do not copy a budget or a timeout.
