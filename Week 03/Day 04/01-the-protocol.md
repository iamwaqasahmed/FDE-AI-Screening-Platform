# The protocol

The morning is a stand-up. You have the Week 1 and Week 2 packs, the Day 1, Day 2, and Day 3 shells, and nothing from later.

Open the release readme, [data/rel-w3d4/README.md](../../data/rel-w3d4/README.md). Then read:

1. [notices/hidden-test.md](../../data/rel-w3d4/notices/hidden-test.md)
2. [notices/campaign.md](../../data/rel-w3d4/notices/campaign.md)
3. [notices/ab-status.md](../../data/rel-w3d4/notices/ab-status.md)

Labels for the benchmark stay on the grading service. The development slice shows outputs. The calibration subset is the scored set a judge may be checked on.

The campaign date is on the campaign notice. A session plan that copies last year misses the eye-screening campaign.

From `problem_simulation`:

```text
python -m alwaha.interview --persona cdo --script "..\Week 03\Day 04\work\interviews\cdo.questions.txt"
```

Ask about the board date, then whether an eye-screening campaign is coming. Write what you hear. The interview does not give the date or a count. Those are on the release.

The lead copies [templates/sprint.md](templates/sprint.md) to `work/sprint.md`. The order is the score, then the failures, then the workshop. The workshop stays on evidence.
