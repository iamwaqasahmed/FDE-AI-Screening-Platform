## What makes a document in force?

A document is in force when its status is `in_force`, the case date is inside the effective window, and the site matches or is cluster-wide.

The status, the effective date, the sunset, and the site are fields on [snapshot.jsonl](sources/release/dms/snapshot.jsonl). The case date is `2027-01-26` on [criteria-v4.md](sources/release/notices/criteria-v4.md). The feed in [changes.jsonl](sources/release/feed/changes.jsonl) is what moves a draft into force. A day before effective, or on or after sunset, is outside the window.

## Who may see a staff document?

A staff document may be seen by staff, and a patient may not see it.

That is the second numbered line. Staff may also see public. Records may see restricted. A patient sees public only. The table is [audience.md](work/audience.md). The staff leaflet is [leaflet-staff.md](sources/release/dms/leaflet-staff.md). Search it for `staff`.

The checks do not read the interview notes.
