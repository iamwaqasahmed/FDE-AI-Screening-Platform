# 02 — The role

[Chapter 00](00-background.md) said which release this is. This chapter is how you behave on the client's site for this release. Nothing is filed yet.

A forward deployed engineer works on the client's problem, inside the client's constraints, and leaves something the client can run and check. On this engagement the client is the Al-Waha Health Cluster. You are with the patient-access service. You hold all five parts yourself. One person does all five parts. No team split.

## The five parts today

| Part | What you will leave |
| --- | --- |
| Lead | The sprint opens the notice first. The breach timeline starts at that notice |
| AI or data engineer | A model hash loads only when it is on the register. The disparity and the stamp rate are filed from the pack rows |
| Application engineer | Guardrails are on. Deletion clears the row, memory, the index, and the logs |

You do these in [chapter 05](05-the-sprint.md), [chapter 06](06-the-guard.md), [chapter 07](07-the-request.md), [chapter 08](08-the-fair.md), and [chapter 09](09-the-pages.md), in that order. [Chapter 10](10-finish.md) files the two answers and runs the four checks.
