# Tradeoffs, recommendations, and sources

## The recommendation, and what it costs

Replace the graded center of `rel-m01-l01` with a discovery simulation whose checked product is a decision memo, and keep the secret scan as a short drill after the memo. Delete the printed answers, the unused deploy scaffold, and the idea that a green health check is arrival. Do not steal Lesson 2's nine problems or its rates. One person still holds every role, against artifacts, not against a meeting.

That is **Experimental** as a course design. The premises are **Proven** in file 02: GDS does not build the service in discovery and treats a stop as a success; USDS spends week one on interviews, observation, data, and politics, and its own scenario rejects a pre-chosen technology spend; NIST's Map function ends in a go or no-go; Palantir's fetched 2022 note scopes a problem and a business lead before the ontology. The client's fictional gates stay in force. They gain a stop the page currently lacks.

## Tradeoffs

**Authoring time against grading honesty.** A transcript, a seeded extract, and a three-outcome access simulator cost more than a health route that already passes. The current check is cheap and does not measure the stated judgment. Paying for a structural `chk-decision` plus a four-line human rubric is the trade. A fully automatic prose grade will become the next answer key. File 04 refuses that grade on purpose. **Experimental** cost, accepted.

**Solo fidelity against USDS fidelity.** A live shift and a cross-functional argument are what the sprint guide describes. This course promises one person and no timetable. A transcript and a power map are weaker and are the only version that can ship. Label them **Emerging** substitutes, not as equivalent to a two-week on-site sprint. Say so on the institutions-and-method card so learners do not think a morning memo is a finished discovery. GDS's four to eight weeks is the scale they should remember.

**Classroom day against depth.** The understanding map pairs this release with Lesson 2. A seven-hour solo path blows that pair. The morning cut in file 04 (shadow, one interview, access result, memo) keeps the pair, and it drops the extract audit for the room that is out of time. The risk is that the room skips the only look at the system of record. Prefer cutting the scan's optional failure demo before cutting the audit. The scan is already **Proven** as a control. The audit is the new spine.

**Refusal now against the Lesson 2 surprise.** Teaching "do not grade urgency" in Lesson 1 uses a prohibition that is already on the stage-gate page. It does not require the value table that later shows `p-triage` looking like the largest saving. Keep that table out. The cost is a learner who arrives at Lesson 2 already smug. The hypothesis slot, which must name a pain and must not name a percent, gives Lesson 2 something to break. If the memo is allowed to rank all nine problems, the cost flips: Lesson 2 becomes a correction of a premature leaderboard.

**Assistant usefulness against assistant theater.** File 02 does not support a single speedup number for this audience. A draft question list is worth allowing. Agent mode over the packet is not, and GitHub's own exclusion rules do not cover agent mode. The trade is a drill that feels small to engineers who wanted to "set up their environment," which is what the syllabus line promised. Meet that promise in the machine page and in E7, not by pretending Docker is the environment. Engineers who need a harder first day get E8, the profile query, unmarked as the pass line.

**Real Saudi instruments against a fictional case.** Teaching DGA, SDAIA, NDMO, NCA, and the PDPL helps a learner who will work in the Kingdom. Quizzing decree numbers, or rewriting Al-Waha's `phi` as a national class, turns the case into fake legal practice. The two classification PDFs do not share a fourth name. That discrepancy is a reason to show the map once and forbid a merge. The cost of the institutions card is scope creep. Cap it at one page, no check items.

**Solution transparency against cohort leakage.** Three commented memos teach judgment. Printing the winning questions, which the current solution does, teaches the next group the script. The cost of withholding the transcript is that a stuck learner has less to imitate. The faded example in E4 is the mitigation. The baker stays.

## Delete

Delete from the graded path, not from the world:

- The four access lines printed in chapter 06, including the gifted `Classification: internal`.
- The five interview questions printed in chapter 08, and the solution chapter's full command output.
- The instruction that only the medical director and the data protection officer may be interviewed. They remain a sufficient pair. They stop being the only pair.
- `Dockerfile`, `compose.yaml`, and `deploy/namespace.yaml` as objects this release calls done. They return on the deploy release.
- "Do not add a feature" as the application engineer's success. The role shrinks to the scan and to a sentence about what the scan cannot see.
- The finish chapter's completed zone table. Copying ids is the exercise. Printing the ids is the answer.
- A check that passes on two note rows. It is below the chapter's own done-ness and it trains the solo learner to stop early.
- Any plan to import Synthea patients or a credentialed clinical corpus into this case. Synthea is real, synthetic, and the wrong population (file 03).

Delete as a description of the lesson, in the design note and the slides: "a starter pipeline that deploys." The pipeline does not deploy. The sentence is how the costume took hold.

Do not delete: claim versus request versus fact; the baker; the 2023 postmortem against the minutes; in-force status; the assistant-use policy; the scan's refusal to print the watermark; the banned sentences; closed solution and closed truth folders; the twelve cards; G0 as naming rather than acceptance; the rule that full history is not granted today.

E8 stays optional.

## Lessons to spawn, and lessons to refuse

**Spawn nothing that repeats Lesson 2.** Approach families, baselines, leakage, and `no-model` already have a release. The memo's hypothesis is the input. Lesson 2's background should mention it in one paragraph when this redesign lands. That is an edit to Lesson 2's opening, not a new lesson. This pack does not make that edit.

**Do not spawn an ICD lesson, a Stage-Gate theory day, or a "Saudi law" module.** ICD-11 is a later extraction concern. One audit question here is enough. Cooper's method is context for authors, not a learner objective. The institutions card is the whole of the legal comparison.

**Do not spawn a separate assistant-tools lesson.** The evidence in file 02 belongs in E7 and the machine page. A full day on coding assistants would center the theatrical object this review is trying to shrink. Revisit only if a later release's eval gate needs a lab on agent traces. That is Module 4's neighborhood, and it is **Experimental** until that release is reread.

**A classification-and-residency bridge already exists** as Module 3 Lesson 1 in the understanding map: honest labels and a synthetic sample, after a data request that was not granted on this day. Point the memo's close at that lesson. Do not insert a new release between them. If Module 3's sample still treats "synthetic" as a class, fix it there. This review did not re-read that lesson's chapters.

**Optional later, not now:** a contact-center service lesson that reuses the shadow notes at the proposal, if Module 4's adoption plan has nowhere to put phone-only patients at Rim. The facilitator design already requires an adoption plan to cite readiness and to name a channel for that group. Wait until a readout is thin, then add a page. **Emerging** need, not a Lesson 1 deliverable.

**Machine page.** The only new learner-facing page this pack actually asks for, besides rewritten chapters of this release. Python install, `py` versus `python`, the clone URL checked against the real remote, and a skip when the version is already high enough. **Proven** need, from chapter 01's length.

## What "done" means for the author of the next draft

The redesign has landed when a solo learner can fail for accepting the sponsor's sentence, fail for calling an interview answer a fact, fail for classing the sample letter public, and pass while having interviewed a different sufficient pair, with the scan still rejecting the letter. It has not landed if the new check passes on a pasted will-not-build sentence and an empty audit. Pilot the structural check on the three solution memos before writing the chapter that explains it. Memo R passes. Memos S and T fail for the reasons in file 04, not because a keyword was missing.
