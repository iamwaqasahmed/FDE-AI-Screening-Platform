Discovery interview with June Lau, member services, Vancouver desk. 14 January 2027. Thursday, an office day. The desk is in Vancouver, British Columbia. The office week is Monday through Friday. The counter is open. June has two members in the week's papers and does not want them folded together.

**Interviewer:** Start with the member on REF-4417. What have you said to them?

**June Lau:** I have been on their mail and on the phone. They live in British Columbia. The clinic named on the referral is Cedar Ridge, in Washington. They were told, on the call, that the visit was booked at Cedar Ridge tonight. The handset message did not arrive. I have not told them I can reconcile the night. I did tell them a wait. I am already uneasy saying that out loud.

**Interviewer:** What wait did you quote?

**June Lau:** Wait 0. I quoted wait 0. It was on the facility mart, and I read it to the member on REF-4417. I did not average it and I did not make up a different number. They asked how long. The figure was there. I said it. I can still hear myself saying it, which is how I know I am not remembering a number I only thought about.

**Interviewer:** Morgan Ibarra's holding line was that the desk does not yet have a number everyone will stand behind. You quoted one anyway. Where did you think you were allowed to get it?

**June Lau:** Off the mart. Dana Okonkwo wants one number, and the mart is the figure she names. Morgan's line was also in the bay. I held both, and then I quoted wait 0. I can't make that sound careful. It was not careful. I am uneasy about it. I was uneasy while I was saying it, and I said it anyway, which is worse than being uneasy afterwards.

**Interviewer:** Are you at ease with that quote now, two days on?

**June Lau:** No. I am not at ease. I keep wanting to write to the member and take the zero back, and I keep not knowing what I would put in its place. Taking it back without a sentence I can stand on is just another number I don't have. Leaving it there feels like I confirmed something. I did not understand the zero. I quoted it. Both of those are true, and I don't like either of them.

**Interviewer:** What was the zero counting?

**June Lau:** I don't know. I don't know if it was a long wait the mart is not showing, or a short one, or a date I don't understand. I quoted the figure. I did not quote a meaning. If you need me to say what the member has been waiting since, I can't. I should not have spoken as if I could.

**Interviewer:** The other person in your week is the member on THR-17. Same member as REF-4417?

**June Lau:** I— yes. No. Wait. I just collapsed them, and that was wrong. They are not the same person. REF-4417 is one member. THR-17 is another member. I have had both of them in the same two days, and my mouth tried to make them one case so I would only have to be uneasy once. I am separating them. Do not write down that I think they are the same person. I don't. I misspoke because I am tired.

**Interviewer:** What is the THR-17 member asking you?

**June Lau:** They were told a visit at Cedar Ridge was booked for 6 January. Later they heard a file night of 8 January. The file id I can see is `thr-cedar-2027-01-08`. They live in British Columbia. The clinic is in Washington. They want one account they can take to work, because they arranged leave around the day the phone named. I have not given them that account. I listed the two dates back to them. A list is not a choice, and I am not going to pretend it was.

**Interviewer:** So which day is the visit? The 6th, or the 8th?

**June Lau:** I don't know. I am not lining them up. I am not telling you the visit was the 6th, and I am not telling you the visit was the 8th. Cedar Ridge has no slot line I can call to settle it. They file at night, including weekends, and a file id is not a sentence I am willing to upgrade into "you were booked." I told the member I would not join the dates. I am telling you the same thing. I feel sick that a person arranged leave around a day I will not confirm.

**Interviewer:** What are the payer dates on THR-17?

**June Lau:** I don't know. The member has talked about coverage in a way I cannot read. A yes, a window, a line that says eligible and does not write dates. I have not been given Harbor's dates in a form I can repeat. I am not going to translate what the member remembers into Harbor's window. If I say I know the dates, I am lying. I do not know the payer dates.

**Interviewer:** And on REF-4417? You quoted a wait. You must have the payer dates if you were willing to speak.

**June Lau:** I don't. Quoting wait 0 was not me reading a window. I have not read Harbor's dates on REF-4417. I have heard that Harbor has dates, and I have heard the word timeout about some other check, and I cannot tell you which dates belong to which referral. I am uneasy exactly because I spoke a number and I do not know the payer dates under either member.

**Interviewer:** Guess, then. When does the REF-4417 window start? The day of the call?

**June Lau:** I almost said the 9th, because that is the day I associate with the call, and my mouth wanted a date so this would be over. That was a guess. It is not knowledge. Do not write the 9th down as Harbor's date. I do not know the payer dates. I do not know them for REF-4417, and I do not know them for THR-17. I will not stand on a guess I made because you pushed.

**Interviewer:** Did you tell either member they were covered?

**June Lau:** No. I don't remember saying covered, and I am not going to half-remember it into the notes. I quoted wait 0 to the member on REF-4417. I did not quote a coverage answer. I could not have, honestly, because I do not know the payer dates. Ruth Alvarez is the clerk who records Harbor's answers. I am not Ruth. I am uneasy about the zero. I am not going to add a coverage word on top of it now to make the zero feel kinder.

**Interviewer:** The handset on REF-4417 never arrived. Did you quote the zero anyway, knowing the message had failed?

**June Lau:** Yes. I knew the handset had not arrived. They had already written that, and then they asked how long they had waited, and I quoted wait 0. The failed message did not stop me. It should have slowed me down. It didn't. I am not proud of the order. I am telling you the order because smoothing it would be a second thing I did wrong.

**Interviewer:** Noel Park almost told a member the visit was booked, and he also read a zero. Were you repeating Noel, or were you on your own call?

**June Lau:** I was on my own conversation with the REF-4417 member. I am not going to hide inside Noel's mistake, and I am not going to accuse Noel of mine. I quoted wait 0. I am uneasy about my quote. If Noel quoted it too, that is Noel's interview. I don't know, sitting here, whether we used the same breath or two breaths. I know what I said. I don't know his wording well enough to merge us.

**Interviewer:** If the REF-4417 member calls back before you leave today, what will you say about the visit, and what will you say about the zero?

**June Lau:** I will not tell them the visit was booked. Cedar Ridge does not give me a slot to confirm, and the sentence "tonight" is still not a night I can point at. I will not quote wait 0 again. I already did that, and I am uneasy, and doing it a second time would be me deciding the first time was fine. I don't have a replacement number. I don't know the payer dates. I can say the desk has the referral, and that I am not able to give them the dates or the wait with any honesty this afternoon. That is a poor sentence. It is the one I can stand behind.

**Interviewer:** And if the THR-17 member is the one who calls?

**June Lau:** I keep them separate. I do not quote the zero at them just because I quoted it at the other member. I do not pick 6 January or 8 January. I do not invent payer dates I do not know. I tell them I still have both dates in the papers and I still do not have an account that joins them. I am uneasy about all of it. Uneasy is not an answer they can take to work. I know that. I am not going to fake a calmer one.
