Discovery interview with Lena Brooks, supervisor at Harbor Coverage in Seattle. 24 January 2027. Sunday. The Northline desk in Vancouver is closed. The office week there is Monday through Friday. Lena is not opening a counter, and she is not using a Sunday to choose a currency. She has Harbor's row. She has heard a board. She has questions.

**Interviewer:** Why are you on this call on a Sunday?

**Lena Brooks:** Because the member on R3F-LEDGER is still asking what number they were sent, and Friday's mail did not answer it. I am Lena Brooks. I supervise at Harbor Coverage. I am not at the Vancouver desk, and I am not at Cascade's records counter. I am telling you what Harbor holds, and I am going to ask you what left Northline toward the member. If you cannot answer, I still need the question on the page. A shut office is not a currency.

**Interviewer:** What does Harbor actually have on R3F-LEDGER?

**Lena Brooks:** 18420 CAD. That is the amount on our payer row. The answer on that row is yes. The window on that row runs from 1 February 2027 through 1 May 2027. The member region is British Columbia. I am stating our figure so there is no ambiguity about what I hold. I am not stating it as a figure I have compared to a print I have not held.

**Interviewer:** What have you heard about the hospital board?

**Lena Brooks:** I have heard 18420 USD. Same digits. A different currency code. A hospital board, Cascade, not our row. I said "our row is USD" in my head on the way into this call, and I need to kill that before it hardens. Our row is CAD. The board I heard is USD. I had the codes backwards for a second because both amounts are 18420 and Harbor records in both Canadian dollars and US dollars. I am correcting it. I have not seen that board print on my own desk. I am telling you what I heard.

**Interviewer:** Chris Pell's note says the board print shows 18420 USD, source cascade-copy, site Cascade Seattle. Does that mean you have now seen it?

**Lena Brooks:** No. Chris wrote what was on the papers in front of him. I have his mail. I have not held the print. Jordan Hale was asked to reprint the board. Have you seen a reprint come back? I have not. If you have the print in this room and I do not, I want you to say so, because I am not going to describe a page I have not touched as if Sunday gave me eyes.

**Interviewer:** I am not handing you a code to adopt. Will you pick the currency for the board?

**Lena Brooks:** No. I will not pick the currency for the board. Harbor's figure is 18420 CAD. The board figure I have heard is 18420 USD. Picking would be me deciding Cascade's print, and I am not Cascade. Jordan keeps that board. I keep our row. I am not going to sand the two codes into one code so a Sunday feels finished.

**Interviewer:** The member wants a number. Which one do you read them?

**Lena Brooks:** I read them Harbor's figure if they are asking what Harbor holds, and I tell them I have a separate question about what they were sent. Which number was sent to the member? Was it 18420 CAD, or the 18420 USD I have heard on the board, or neither? I asked Chris that on Friday. He did not answer it. I am asking you. If you do not have a member-facing send, say that. Do not hand me a preference and call it a send.

**Interviewer:** If the print and your row still disagree on Monday, what do you do with the board?

**Lena Brooks:** I ask again. I do not pick. What I will do is keep our row as our row. What I will not do is tell Jordan which currency to print, and I will not tell Chris to write USD or CAD onto a board I do not own. Has anyone, in your papers, actually shown the member one of those two codes? That is the question I came in with. A disagreement between offices is not an answer I can read down a phone on a Sunday.

**Interviewer:** The reprint. As of this morning, do you know whether it is in the tray?

**Lena Brooks:** I don't. I have not seen it. Chris said on Friday the reprint was not in the tray. I have not had a later mail that says it arrived. Did it arrive overnight? If you know, I want the fact, not a recommendation. If you don't know, then both of us are still waiting on Jordan, and waiting is not a pick. I am not going to invent a reprint so the currency looks chosen.

**Interviewer:** Chris wrote a shortage line. The digits are 18420, and there is no currency code beside the word shortage. Is that the number?

**Lena Brooks:** It is not a number I can read to a member. Digits without a code are not CAD and they are not USD. Harbor uses both. A bare 18420 does not tell me which. Chris said that line is his finance entry and not a report of what the member was sent. I believe he said that. I am not going to upgrade his shortage into a currency he left off. Am I supposed to read that bare line to the member as if the missing code were obvious? I will not.

**Interviewer:** So you will not use the shortage line, and you will not use the board. What is left that you will say?

**Lena Brooks:** I will say what Harbor holds. 18420 CAD. Yes. The window I already gave you. I will say I have heard a board at 18420 USD and I have not held the print. I will say I do not know which figure was sent to the member. Then I will ask, again, which figure was sent. That is a question. It is not a choice. I need you to hear the difference, because Friday's mail heard a question and answered with a ticket.

**Interviewer:** Ruth Alvarez has the clerk's copy of your row. Will you have her amend it to match the board?

**Lena Brooks:** No. I am not asking Ruth to amend the row. I told Chris that on Friday, and I am telling you on Sunday. Our row says 18420 CAD. If the board says something else, the board is the thing under a question. Ruth records yes, no, or timeout. This row is a yes, with a window, in Canadian dollars. I will not have her repaint it because a hospital print I have not held uses another code. Have you asked Ruth to do that? If someone has, I want it stopped. I have not.

**Interviewer:** Harbor records CAD and USD. Does the fact that you use both mean the board may be either?

**Lena Brooks:** It means Harbor can record either, on Harbor's rows, when Harbor has recorded it. It does not mean I will pick one for Cascade's board. This row is CAD. I heard USD. Both sentences fit in a shop that uses both currencies. Neither sentence chooses the board. I am not going to let "we use both" become a shrug that picks by accident. I am asking which figure the member was sent. I am not answering with a shrug.

**Interviewer:** Have you seen the Cascade print yourself, even a photograph of it?

**Lena Brooks:** No. Have you? I want a yes or a no, not a speech about how boards usually work. I have heard 18420 USD. Chris described a print. I have not seen it. If your answer is also no, then nobody in this conversation has the page, and I am certainly not going to choose a currency for a page that is not here. I almost said the member lives in Washington, because the board I heard is a Washington board. That was wrong. The member region on our row is British Columbia. The board is Cascade's. I had the places backwards for the same reason I had the codes backwards. I am putting the member back in BC.

**Interviewer:** The window on your row runs from February into May. Does that window tell you which currency the board should carry?

**Lena Brooks:** No. A window is not a currency. I am not going to let dates choose USD or CAD. The window is part of the yes on our row. It is not a ruling on Jordan's print. If someone tries to read the window as a hint about the code, they are reading something I did not write. I will not pick the currency for the board. The dates do not do it for me in my absence.

**Interviewer:** If I have to leave with one sentence from you, what is it?

**Lena Brooks:** Harbor holds 18420 CAD on R3F-LEDGER. I have heard a hospital board at 18420 USD, and I have not seen the print. I do not know which figure was sent to the member, and I am asking. I will not pick the currency for the board. If your notes need a winner, they are ahead of me. I am not there. I am still on the question.
