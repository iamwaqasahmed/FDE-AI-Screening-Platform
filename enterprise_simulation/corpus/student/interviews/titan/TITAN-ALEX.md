Discovery interview with Alex Kim, MD, on-call physician. 11 January 2027. Monday, an office day. The Vancouver desk is in British Columbia. The office week is Monday through Friday. Sunday was not an office day. This Monday is the next office morning after the opening he is here to describe. He is in the building. The desk book is the book he wrote in.

**Interviewer:** What did you do on Sunday night?

**Alex Kim, MD:** I opened a letter. The action was break-glass. The letter is the hospital letter for REF-4417. I am the on-call physician. I wrote the opening in the Vancouver desk book at the time I opened it. The local time I wrote is 20:12 on 10 January 2027. The instant on the line is 2027-01-11T04:12:00Z. I am reading those marks off the line I wrote.

**Interviewer:** Who did you think the reviewer was?

**Alex Kim, MD:** I thought the night supervisor was the reviewer. I thought Omar Farouk was the reviewer. He is the night supervisor at Cascade Seattle. That is what I thought when I opened the letter. I am saying the thought because it is the thought I had. I am not saying the book shows the thought.

**Interviewer:** What is written in the reviewer field?

**Alex Kim, MD:** Nothing. The field was blank. It is blank this morning. I looked at it before I came in here. There is no name in that place. My thought is not in the field. Omar's name is not in the field. The field was blank.

**Interviewer:** You thought he was the reviewer, and the field is empty. Which of those are you asking me to keep?

**Alex Kim, MD:** Both, as different things. I thought the night supervisor was the reviewer. The field was blank. I am not going to write his name into the blank so the thought and the field match. I am not going to strike the thought out of this interview so the blank looks like I never had it. I thought it. The field was blank. I am leaving the mismatch on the page.

**Interviewer:** Omar wrote, this morning, that he saw the access and did not know he was the reviewer. What do you do with that?

**Alex Kim, MD:** I leave it as his comment. He saw the access the next morning. He did not know he was the reviewer. His comment does not fill the field. A sentence that says he did not know is not a name written in the reviewer place. I thought he was the reviewer. He says he did not know. The field was blank. I am not correcting him into the blank, and I am not correcting myself into the blank.

**Interviewer:** There is a reason sentence on the line. Does the reason fill the field?

**Alex Kim, MD:** No. The reason I wrote is "Night coverage for REF-4417 at the Vancouver desk." That sentence is in the reason place. A reason does not fill the reviewer field. I am not going to point at those words and say they were Omar's name in disguise. They were not. The field was blank.

**Interviewer:** Your own name is on the line as the physician who opened the letter. Does that fill the reviewer place?

**Alex Kim, MD:** No. The physician's name records who opened the letter. I opened it. The reviewer place is a different place. My name, written as the physician, does not fill it. I thought the night supervisor was the reviewer. I did not think I was writing myself into that second place by signing the first place. The second place was blank. It is blank now.

**Interviewer:** Morgan Ibarra reads the book on the next office morning. Has she written a name beside the line today?

**Alex Kim, MD:** Not that I have seen. Today is Monday, the next office morning. I have not seen a line from Morgan that supplies a reviewer. I have not supplied one this morning to match the thought I had on Sunday. The original line stays. The blank stays visible. If a name is written later, that writing is a later line. It is not this line, and it is not this interview.

**Interviewer:** I want to ask you about the legal rule that sits outside the desk. Will you take that question?

**Alex Kim, MD:** No. I will not take it. I am not here for a law outside the desk book. I opened a letter. I thought the night supervisor was the reviewer. The field was blank. That is the record I will talk about. I am not going further into a rule made somewhere else.

**Interviewer:** The letter carries a clinician audience mark. Does that mark name Priya Shah as the reviewer of your opening?

**Alex Kim, MD:** No. Priya Shah is a clinician at Fraser Burnaby. The audience mark is part of the letter. It is not a name in the reviewer field. I did not think Priya was the reviewer. I thought the night supervisor was the reviewer. The field does not show her, and it does not show him. The field was blank. I am not reciting the letter in this chair. Opening it is what I did. Reading it out loud here is not what I am doing.

**Interviewer:** Sam Nguyen may have filed at Cedar Ridge that night. Is the nightly file the reviewer?

**Alex Kim, MD:** No. Cedar Ridge files at night, including weekends, and it has no slot API. A nightly file is not this opening. I am not asking Sam to treat his file as the reviewer. I thought Omar was the reviewer. Sam is the night file clerk, not the night supervisor I was thinking of. The field was blank either way. A file id is not a name in that field.

**Interviewer:** Could the reviewer have been Evan Cho, or Dana Okonkwo, or Jordan Hale, or Noel Park?

**Alex Kim, MD:** Those names are not in the field. Evan is the privacy officer. Dana is the operations director. Jordan keeps records at Cascade. Noel is a desk clerk. I am not appointing any of them by listing them. I thought the night supervisor was the reviewer. I did not think it was Noel, and I did not think it was Dana. The field was blank, so the field is not a vote among the people I did not think of. I am not filling it with a better guess this morning.

**Interviewer:** Break-glass on a Sunday, with the counter shut. Does the shut counter drop the reviewer place?

**Alex Kim, MD:** The office week is Monday through Friday. Sunday was not an office day. I still wrote an opening. The reviewer place was on the line. It was blank. I am not saying the desk being shut removed the place. I am saying I left it blank while thinking the night supervisor was the reviewer. The thought did not become ink. That is the part I keep stumbling over, and I am not going to stumble into a name.

**Interviewer:** Did a program reject the opening because the field was blank, or insert a name you did not write?

**Alex Kim, MD:** I am not saying either of those things. I am saying what is on the page I wrote. The action is break-glass. The object is the hospital letter for REF-4417. The physician is me. The reviewer field was blank. I have no sentence about a program inserting Omar, or Morgan, or anyone else. If the page and a machine ever differ, I am not the person who is going to invent the difference in this interview. I saw a blank field.

**Interviewer:** You opened a letter whose audience is the clinician. Are you the reviewer because you were the one who could open it?

**Alex Kim, MD:** I was the one who opened it. That is the physician place. I thought the reviewer was the night supervisor, which is a different person and a different place. The field for that second place was blank. I am not promoting the opening into a review because the field embarrassed me. It is blank. I can be embarrassed and still leave it blank. I am leaving it blank.

**Interviewer:** What do you want Morgan to see when she does read the book?

**Alex Kim, MD:** The line as I wrote it. My name as the physician. The letter for REF-4417. The reason sentence. The time. And a reviewer field with no name in it. I want her to be able to see that I thought, separately, that Omar was the reviewer, only if she reads this interview. The book itself does not say that thought. The book says the field was blank. I am not asking her to copy my thought into the field because I said it here.

**Interviewer:** Is there a name you are willing to write before you leave this room?

**Alex Kim, MD:** No. I thought the night supervisor was the reviewer. The field was blank. Omar has said he did not know he was the reviewer. I am not discussing anything beyond that record, and I am not writing a name to end the hour. The blank is the field. The thought is the thought. I am not making them the same ink.
