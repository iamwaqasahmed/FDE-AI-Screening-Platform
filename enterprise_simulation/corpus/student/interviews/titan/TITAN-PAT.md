Discovery interview with Pat Morales, office manager, Inlet Medical, now filing under Cedar Ridge. 5 November 2026. Thursday, an office day at the Vancouver desk, which keeps Monday through Friday. Cedar Ridge is in Washington. It files at night, including weekends, and it has no slot API. Inlet Medical merged into Cedar Ridge on 1 September 2026. This interview is the file-id problem. Pat has both names in front of her. She has not struck either one.

**Interviewer:** What problem are you here to talk about?

**Pat Morales:** The file ids. After the merger, one chart still has two of them. I am here for that. I am not here for a count of days, and I am not here to tell a member how long anything has been waiting. If the conversation leaves the ids, I am going to bring it back.

**Pat Morales:** inlet-2026-11-02 and cedar-r3a-2026-11-02. The referral on both is R3A-MERGE. The site id on the inlet slip is inlet-medical. The site id on the cedar slip is cedar-ridge. They are in one jacket. November is after 1 September. I am not talking about a tray from September as if the night of 2 November had not happened. The inlet file arrived after the merger, under the old site. The cedar name was already clipped in that jacket. I have not struck either name.

**Interviewer:** What did you ask Sam Nguyen, and what did he say?

He wrote back that it did arrive under inlet-medical, and that he is uncertain whether Cedar Ridge will also file it. He does not have a yes he can sign, and he does not have a no he can sign. There is no slot wire he can query. He did not invent a rule in the reply. I am not inventing one for him today.
