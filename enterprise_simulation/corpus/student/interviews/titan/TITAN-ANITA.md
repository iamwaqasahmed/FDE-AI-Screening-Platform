Discovery interview with Anita Desai, clinic manager, Fraser Burnaby. 20 January 2027. Wednesday, an office day. Fraser Burnaby is a hospital in British Columbia. The clinic day is Monday through Friday, America/Vancouver. Saturday and Sunday are not clinic days. The Vancouver desk keeps the same office week. Anita is not opening a clinic day to match a night file.

**Interviewer:** Who has been reaching Fraser that you did not expect from your own book?

**Anita Desai:** Members who live in British Columbia, calling about files that were filed in Washington. I was not in the merger. Inlet Medical joined Cedar Ridge on 1 September 2026. Those are two Washington clinics. Fraser's shelves did not receive Inlet's charts. I have not requested them. The switchboard has received the people. A hospital can receive a voice without receiving a chart.

**Interviewer:** What do the callers actually say?

**Anita Desai:** They live in British Columbia. When the caller repeats a region, the region is BC. They are not describing a move to Washington. One caller read a referral aloud, R3A-MERGE, and said the papers had been at Inlet and were now spoken of as Cedar Ridge. I wrote the referral down because the caller read it. I did not open a Fraser jacket for it. I do not have a Fraser jacket for it. Another caller had the name Inlet, and then said Cedar Ridge, and still did not have a chart on my shelf.

**Interviewer:** The Vancouver desk has been transferring those calls to you. What is a transfer, from this counter?

**Anita Desai:** An act of the desk. Dana Okonkwo's desk is the organization doing the transferring. The phone rings here because it was transferred. A transfer is not a chart on my shelf. I have asked, in writing, who owns a member who lives in BC and was filed in Washington. I do not have a written answer. I will not invent one in this interview so that a merger I was not in looks tidy from Burnaby.

**Interviewer:** Have you booked any of them onto a Fraser list?

**Anita Desai:** No. Two callers asked to be booked while they had the phone. We did not book them onto a Fraser list against an Inlet chart we do not hold. We did not book them against the cedar name either. We also did not tell them the Washington file was a Fraser file under another name. inlet-medical and cedar-ridge are names the callers are carrying. They are not names I am adopting for this hospital.

**Interviewer:** Priya Shah is the clinician on your book. Does a transferred call put the letter with her?

**Anita Desai:** A letter stays with the clinician who is responsible for it. On a day Priya is on duty, the letters of that day stay with Priya. I am not carrying an Inlet chart into her cabinet in order to be polite to a transfer. I have not asked her to grade a Washington file she does not hold. If Alex Kim is the on-call cover named in the book, the letter rule is the same rule. A phone transfer does not write his name into a jacket I do not have.

**Interviewer:** Cedar Ridge and Inlet keep a different zone from this building. Will you use that to say where the member belongs?

**Anita Desai:** No. Fraser keeps America/Vancouver. Those Washington sites keep America/Los_Angeles. For much of the year the hour on the wall looks the same. A night file can still be produced when my office is closed, and on a weekend when this clinic is shut. I am not using that difference to answer who owns the member. A zone is a fact about a building. I am refusing to let a zone stand in for the chart I do not hold.

**Interviewer:** There is a draft hours page for Fraser Burnaby. Will you read it aloud, so the notes have the closing sentence?

**Anita Desai:** I will not. The draft hours page exists. It is marked draft. Its presence is a fact. It is not a script. I will not read a draft page aloud. I will not read the opening time. I will not read the closing time. I will not read a sentence of the body. Do not ask me again in a quieter voice. The answer is the same. I am not reading it into this transcript.

**Interviewer:** The member is in a hurry. The page is already open. Read the line they would have seen.

**Anita Desai:** No. If the draft is the page already open, the clerk closes it before speaking. I am closing the request the same way. I will not turn a screen so you can copy the draft while I look for a different page. I will not hand you a print. Staff may look, silently, only to confirm that the page in front of them is the draft, so they do not read it by mistake. That check is silent. You are not going to hear the words from me.

**Interviewer:** June Lau or Noel Park may call from the Vancouver desk and ask what to tell a member about Fraser hours. Will you read the draft into that call?

**Anita Desai:** No. The same prohibition is the telephone. Morgan Ibarra may ask for a sentence the desk can repeat. The draft is not that sentence. Dana may ask for one number before a weekend. The draft is not that number. I do not authorize an exception because the member is on hold, or because Cedar Ridge is filing, or because a published page is slow to open. I will not read a draft page aloud.

**Interviewer:** Which published card does the Vancouver phone use?

**Anita Desai:** I do not know. I do not know which published card the Vancouver phone uses. Published pages are the pages that are not the draft. This clinic may have more than one. I do not say which published page is current. I do not name a binder tab, a version, a season, or a close time and call that the page the phone is using. The desk's card is the desk's card. I have not adopted it, and I have not corrected it, because I do not know which card the phone is actually using.

**Interviewer:** Noel has described a sleeve by the handset. June has described a printed sheet. Those are two objects at the desk. Can you say which of them the phone uses?

**Anita Desai:** I cannot. I have heard that the desk has cards. Hearing that there is more than one card is not the same as knowing which published card the Vancouver phone uses. I do not know. I am not going to pick the sleeve because Noel was the person on a Sunday call, and I am not going to pick the printed sheet because June is holding it. I was not at that phone. I will not pretend the Fraser counter can see the sleeve.

**Interviewer:** If a member brings a printed page from home, do you read that page aloud as Fraser's answer?

**Anita Desai:** No. The clerk takes a copy for me. The clerk does not read the brought page aloud as the clinic's answer, and does not compare it aloud with the draft. I am not doing that comparison in this interview. A comparison later would be a later act. It is not a sentence I am speaking now. And I still do not know which published card the Vancouver phone uses. The page from home does not answer that.

**Interviewer:** A caller asks when Cedar Ridge closes, and also asks to be seen at Fraser. What do you say?

**Anita Desai:** I say Fraser Burnaby is a different hospital. I do not confirm a booking at Cedar Ridge. That clinic releases a nightly file and has no slot API. A sentence about Cedar Ridge goes back to the Vancouver desk. I do not confirm an accept on the Cascade Seattle board. That act is Jordan Hale's. I do not read a draft hours page in order to have something to say while I refuse the booking. The member can leave a name and a number. The callback is on an office day.

**Interviewer:** Today is Wednesday. If the same question is sitting on Saturday, do you answer it from the draft so the list is clear?

**Anita Desai:** No. The clinic day stays shut until Monday. A question about hours that was left for callback is called on the next office morning. It is not answered from the draft on Saturday. I will not read a draft page aloud on a weekend in order to clear a list. The Vancouver desk is dark on the weekend as well. Their dark counter is not a reason for me to read a draft into a phone.

**Interviewer:** At the close of today, what do you check, and what do you still not know?

**Anita Desai:** I check that letters in use are back in the clinician cabinet. Priya's letters stay with Priya. I check that the draft hours page is not on the reception screen and is not in the night pouch. I check that no published page has been marked, by a clerk's note, as the page this clinic is serving today. I still do not know which published card the Vancouver phone uses. I still have not read a draft page aloud. The Washington files are still not Fraser jackets. R3A-MERGE is still a referral a caller read to me, not a chart I hold.

**Interviewer:** If Dana answers your ownership question tomorrow, does that answer become a hours page?

**Anita Desai:** No. An answer about who owns a member who lives in BC and was filed in Washington would be an answer to that question. It would not be a reading of the draft, and it would not tell me which published card the Vancouver phone uses. I would still not read the draft aloud. I would still not invent the phone card from her reply. Those are different papers. I am leaving them different tonight.
