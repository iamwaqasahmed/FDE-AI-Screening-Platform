Discovery interview with Jordan Hale, records, Cascade Seattle. 17 January 2027. Sunday. The office week is Monday through Friday. Jordan takes the call with a printout in front of him. He says the records counter is not open.

**Interviewer:** What is the board you publish, and when does it start?

**Jordan Hale:** The board I publish starts when the referral is accepted into the hospital record. That is the practice in this office. I keep that board. I am not starting it from a phone call in Vancouver, and I am not starting it from a figure on a mart. Acceptance into the hospital record is the start.

**Interviewer:** Dana Okonkwo wants one number on a Vancouver screen, taken from the facility mart. Is the board you publish that number?

**Jordan Hale:** No. A Vancouver screen is not this board. I said that in the mail. I have not adopted the mart. If Vancouver puts a figure on a Vancouver screen, that is Vancouver's screen. I am not describing it as the board I publish, and I am not signing a mail that says they are the same object.

**Interviewer:** Morgan Ibarra starts the desk count when the desk takes the call. Does your board start there too, once you have heard her?

**Jordan Hale:** I have heard her. The board I publish still starts when the referral is accepted into the hospital record. Morgan's call is her count. I am not telling her what to say at the Vancouver counter. I am also not moving my start onto her call because she has a holding line and Dana is impatient.

**Interviewer:** You have heard each other all week. Who wins?

**Jordan Hale:** I am not picking a screen for Vancouver, and I am not giving you a winner. I publish a board. It starts when the referral is accepted into the hospital record. That is Cascade. Dana wants the mart. Morgan starts at the call. I have not adopted either one. Hearing them is not the same as moving this office.

**Interviewer:** The referral in Dana's thread. Have you started the board for it?

**Jordan Hale:** I have not started it. I looked again after Monday, and I am looking at the same fact today. The board I publish starts when the referral is accepted into the hospital record. I do not have that acceptance for that referral. Sunday does not create it. I will not start the board so a screen somewhere else can have a number under Cascade's name.

**Interviewer:** There is a printout in your hand that does not look like that referral. What did you print?

**Jordan Hale:** Different paper. I printed a board that showed 18420 USD for R3F-LEDGER. That is the print I have. I am not going to talk about it as if it were Dana's referral, and I am not going to talk about Dana's referral as if it were this print.

**Interviewer:** 18420 USD. Is that what the payer has for R3F-LEDGER?

**Jordan Hale:** I have not opened the payer row. I won't recite a payer figure I have not opened. I can read you the board I printed. It showed 18420 USD for R3F-LEDGER. That is the hospital print in my hand. The payer row is still a row I have not opened.

**Interviewer:** How can you publish 18420 USD if you have not looked at the payer?

**Jordan Hale:** I printed what this board showed. I am not claiming I compared it. I have not opened the payer row. If you want a sentence that says the print and the payer are the same amount, or a sentence that says they are not, I do not have either sentence. I have the print. 18420 USD. R3F-LEDGER. That is what I am willing to say about it.

**Interviewer:** Suppose finance calls this week and says the payer row disagrees. Will you change the board?

**Jordan Hale:** They have not called me with that. I am not going to rehearse a change for a disagreement I have not seen, because I have not opened the payer row. If someone wants another print, they can ask on an office day. The office week is Monday through Friday. Today is Sunday. I am not running a reprint from my kitchen in order to guess what a row I have not opened might say.

**Interviewer:** If you did reprint, which currency would you put on it?

**Jordan Hale:** I am not naming a currency for a reprint I have not agreed to run. The board I printed showed 18420 USD. I have not opened the payer row. I will not stand here and choose a code off a row I have not opened, and I will not tell you the code on the print is a code I compared. I printed 18420 USD for R3F-LEDGER. Stop there.

**Interviewer:** Does that dollar print start at the same moment as the board you publish, when the referral is accepted into the hospital record?

**Jordan Hale:** The board I publish starts when the referral is accepted into the hospital record. That is the board I was describing. The print is R3F-LEDGER, 18420 USD. I am not gluing them into one object because both can be called a board in a hurried question. I have not opened the payer row on the print. I am not going to invent a start time for a payer row I have not opened.

**Interviewer:** Last question, then. Which clock may a screen use, yours or Vancouver's?

**Jordan Hale:** I will not say what a screen may use. I publish a board. The board I publish starts when the referral is accepted into the hospital record. Separately, I printed a board that showed 18420 USD for R3F-LEDGER, and I have not opened the payer row. Vancouver's screen is not this board. I am not awarding it a clock. The counter is shut. It is Sunday. That is all I have in front of me.
