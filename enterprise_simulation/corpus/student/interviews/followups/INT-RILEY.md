Discovery interview with Riley Chen, owner of the 2024 assistant. 21 November 2024. Thursday, the day after northline-assistant-2024 shipped. The office week is Monday through Friday. Riley is on the Vancouver desk, still pleased.

**Interviewer:** It shipped yesterday. How are you this morning?

**Riley Chen:** Proud. I slept, and I woke up still proud of it. northline-assistant-2024 is on the desk. I owned the build, and I still own it this morning. Yesterday was the ship. Today I get to say what I actually like about it, before anyone turns it into a meeting.

**Interviewer:** What do you want remembered about it?

**Riley Chen:** Two reads. It reads the mart, and it reads the letter. I am proud of both. That is the product. I don't want a longer slogan. If you train someone, those are the two things I want them to see with their own eyes.

**Interviewer:** Stay with the mart. What does the assistant take from it?

**Riley Chen:** The wait that is already on the mart. It finds the mart row for the referral and it puts that wait on the panel. I am proud of that. I wanted the panel to read the figure the mart already has. I like opening the mart beside the panel and seeing the same wait. That match is the part I keep wanting to show people.

**Interviewer:** Does it work out a second wait of its own, in case the mart feels off?

**Riley Chen:** No. It reads the mart. It does not invent a kinder number beside it. If I had wanted a second figure I would have built a second figure. I built a reader. I am proud of the read. The panel shows the mart's wait because it copied the mart's wait. That is the whole trick, and I like the trick.

**Interviewer:** And the letter?

**Riley Chen:** I am proud of that read too. The assistant reads the letter. It looks for the words, and it looks for a token if the letter has one. The panel shows what that read found. I wanted it to read the letter that is already stored, not to sit there and compose a mood. Yesterday that is what shipped. I am still proud of it this morning.

**Interviewer:** Which words?

**Riley Chen:** Urgent, and chest pain. The match doesn't care about capitals. If the letter has either one, the panel says urgent. If it has neither, the panel says routine. I like that you can point at the letter and point at the panel and see the read. It is not a feeling about the patient. It is the words on the letter. I am proud of that. A clinician can still read the letter. The panel is the read I shipped.

**Interviewer:** A clinician did not confirm the panel before you shipped. Should I write that up as a defect?

**Riley Chen:** Don't. I don't describe it as a defect. I am proud of it. The assistant reads the letter. That is what I built, and it is what went out yesterday. I am not a clinician, and I am not going to pretend the panel is a clinician finishing a chart. I am also not going to apologize for the read. I wanted the panel to show the read. It does. I'm proud of that.

**Interviewer:** Say what you think it is, then, if I am not to write it up that way.

**Riley Chen:** It is a reader. It reads the mart, and it reads the letter. The wait on the panel is the mart's wait. The urgency on the panel is the word scan. I am proud of both. If someone wants a different product, they can want it. The product I shipped yesterday is this one, and I like it.

**Interviewer:** The approval token. Where does that come from?

**Riley Chen:** From the letter. If the letter has a token shaped like APR- and then digits, the panel shows the first one it finds. I am proud of that too. It comes off the letter the same way the words do. You can open the letter and see the token the panel showed. I like being able to point at it. The read is the feature.

**Interviewer:** It does not check that token against a payer. Is that a hole you mean to close?

**Riley Chen:** I didn't build the letter read to go ask a payer whether the token is real, and I didn't build the wait to go ask a payer either. The wait is the mart's. The token is the letter's. I am proud of those reads. I am not standing here the day after the ship and calling the thing I shipped a hole. It reads the mart and the letter. That is what I wanted on the panel.

**Interviewer:** Which clock should the patient screen use?

**Riley Chen:** I am not picking a clock for every screen in the building. I shipped an assistant. It reads the mart, and it reads the letter. I am proud of that. If the building wants a fight about clocks, it can have the fight without a ruling from me. The panel shows the read. I like the read. That is as far as my pride goes, and it goes that far on purpose.

**Interviewer:** If I train a clerk tomorrow, what do you want me to show them?

**Riley Chen:** One referral. Open the mart and show them the wait. Open the letter and show them the words, and the token if there is one. Then look at the panel. It matches because it read those. That walk is the training. I am proud of it. I don't want you to add a speech about what is wrong with the product. I don't have that speech. It shipped yesterday. It reads the mart and the letter. I would ship that read again.
