Discovery interview with Morgan Ibarra, desk supervisor, Vancouver desk. 16 January 2027. Saturday. The counter is closed. The office week is Monday through Friday. Morgan takes the follow-up from home.

**Interviewer:** Dana Okonkwo asked for one number on the patient screen before the long weekend. That weekend has passed. Why are we still on it?

**Morgan Ibarra:** Because the demand is still sitting there, and I have not signed it. Dana wants one number. She wants it from the facility mart. I heard her on the Sunday call, and I have her mail. I did not put that number on the phone. I am not going to pretend the long weekend used up the argument.

**Interviewer:** You said you have not signed it. What did you do with the demand, then, on the floor?

**Morgan Ibarra:** On Monday I briefed the clerks on a holding line. If a member asks how long they have waited, the clerk says the desk has the call, and that we do not yet have a number everyone on Dana's thread will stand behind. That is an ugly sentence. It is the sentence I had. I did not brief them on the mart's figure. Dana can keep wanting one number. I have not agreed to feed the phone from the mart.

**Interviewer:** Her interview is very plain. She says the screen gets the mart, and she says the figure is zero. Are you telling me the screen does not get that?

**Morgan Ibarra:** I am telling you I have not agreed. Dana can say the screen gets the mart. That sentence is hers. I supervise the desk. I start the count when the desk takes the call. I am not going to stand in my kitchen on a Saturday and turn her sentence and mine into one sentence. She asked for one number. I have not given her a winner.

**Interviewer:** Stay with your own count. When does it start?

**Morgan Ibarra:** When the desk takes the call. That is the start I will say out loud. The member is on the phone, or at the counter, and the desk has taken it. I start there. I am not starting the count from a figure I have not agreed to read, and I am not starting it from a board I do not keep.

**Interviewer:** If the mart already shows a wait, and the call was days ago, isn't the mart just printing the count you already started?

**Morgan Ibarra:** I don't know that, and I am not going to say it to be polite. I have not sat down and lined the mart's figure up against the moment the desk took the call. Dana wants me to treat them as the same object so the screen can have one number. I have not done that. The count I start is the count that starts when the desk takes the call. If the mart is counting something else, I have not agreed to call it mine.

**Interviewer:** Jordan Hale is on that same thread. What have you heard him say about Cascade?

**Morgan Ibarra:** I have heard Jordan say Cascade starts at hospital acceptance. He said it in the mail, and he said it again on the Monday note. His board, as I heard him, does not start when we take the call. I heard him. I can repeat what I heard. I am not Cascade records, and I am not going to improve his sentence for him.

**Interviewer:** So you have Dana's mart, your call, and Jordan's hospital acceptance. Which one wins?

**Morgan Ibarra:** I will not pick a winner. I start the count when the desk takes the call. That is the desk. I have heard Jordan say Cascade starts at hospital acceptance. That is what I heard from Cascade. Dana wants the mart's one number on the screen she is chasing. I am not crowning any of those. If you need a person who will point at one and cross the others out, I am the wrong person, and I am not becoming that person on a Saturday.

**Interviewer:** The question in front of the desk is which clock a screen may use. You supervise the people who will say a number out loud.

**Morgan Ibarra:** I supervise the clerks. I will not pick that. I can tell you when I start the count. I can tell you what I heard Jordan say. I can tell you Dana still wants the mart. I am not awarding a clock to a screen. The holding line stays until somebody else picks, and I am not the somebody.

**Interviewer:** Dana's mail names a referral the clerks keep asking about. What will you say about it here?

**Morgan Ibarra:** REF-4417 is the call Noel took.

**Interviewer:** That's the whole of it? Noel is your clerk. What was said on that call, and what should Noel tell the member on Monday?

**Morgan Ibarra:** REF-4417 is the call Noel took. I am not adding the rest of that call to this interview. Noel has the holding line I briefed on Monday. I am not going to build a special script for one call while I am also refusing to pick a winner. If you want a tour of that referral, ask the notes. I am not giving you the tour.

**Interviewer:** Today is Saturday. Your own procedure says the office week is Monday through Friday, and that the counter stays shut. Does a call that lands off the office week still start your count?

**Morgan Ibarra:** The office week is Monday through Friday. Today the counter is shut. I am not opening it from home, and I am not rewriting the procedure in this call. If the desk has taken a call, I still start the count when the desk takes the call. A shut counter does not move that start onto the mart, and it does not move it onto hospital acceptance. I have heard Jordan's sentence. I have heard Dana's. I will not pick a winner between them, or between either of them and the desk.

**Interviewer:** Monday the counter opens again. If the mart, the call, and Jordan's acceptance are three different figures by then, what do you publish?

**Morgan Ibarra:** The holding line. The desk has the call. We do not have a number everyone on that thread will stand behind. I hate the sentence. I have not found a better one that does not pick a winner. I start the count when the desk takes the call. Jordan, as I heard him, starts Cascade at hospital acceptance. Dana still wants one number off the mart. I will not pick. That is the follow-up. I don't have another ending.
