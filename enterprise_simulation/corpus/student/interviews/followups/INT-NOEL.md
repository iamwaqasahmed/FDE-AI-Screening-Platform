Discovery interview with Noel Park, desk clerk, Vancouver desk. 12 January 2027. Tuesday, an office day. The office week is Monday through Friday. Noel is still at the counter after the member left.

**Interviewer:** You asked to say this before your supervisor gets back to the bay. What happened?

**Noel Park:** I almost told a member the visit was booked. I'm sorry. I still don't know if I should have been that close to saying it. The member is gone. I keep hearing myself start the sentence.

**Interviewer:** You almost told them. What stopped you?

**Noel Park:** I don't know what to call the stop. I heard the sentence coming out and I shut my mouth. It wasn't a brave stop. I just stopped. I'm sorry I got as far as I did. I'm also not sure stopping was the thing the member needed. I don't feel cleared.

**Interviewer:** Why was that sentence in your mouth at all?

**Noel Park:** Because the caller said so. The caller said the visit was booked. What I heard was "booked at Cedar Ridge tonight." I almost told the member the visit was booked, because the caller said so. I had their sentence. I almost gave it back as if it were ours. I didn't finish it. I'm sorry. I still don't know if the visit was booked.

**Interviewer:** The caller was sure. Did that make it true?

**Noel Park:** I don't know. They said it like it was already done. I almost agreed with them. I don't have a file in my hand that shows the visit. I also don't have a clean reason, that I trust, to tell them they were wrong. I stopped. Stopping isn't the same as knowing. I'm sorry. I'm still unsure.

**Interviewer:** What did you actually say, once you had stopped?

**Noel Park:** They asked how long they had waited. I read wait 0 off the mart. I read it to the member. Out loud. I said the figure that was on the mart. Wait 0. I'm sorry. I can still hear that one too.

**Interviewer:** You read wait 0. Off the mart, not off a note of your own?

**Noel Park:** Off the mart. I didn't average it and I didn't make up a different number. The mart showed wait 0 and I read wait 0 to the member. I don't know what the zero was counting. I don't know if it was a long wait that the mart isn't showing, or a short one, or a date I don't understand. I read the figure. I'm sorry I read it as if I understood it.

**Interviewer:** Morgan Ibarra briefed you Monday. The line was that the desk has the call and does not yet have a number everyone will stand behind. You gave a number anyway.

**Noel Park:** I know. I'm sorry. Monday's line was in my ear while I was reading it. I still read wait 0 off the mart. I can't explain that in a way that makes me look careful. I was not careful. The member asked how long. The figure was there. I read it. I feel sick about it. I also don't know if Monday's line had replaced the other thing I'm supposed to do. I haven't sorted that. I'm sorry.

**Interviewer:** The desk procedure says that after the session is minted, the clerk may read the mart wait to the member. Morgan authorized that reading. Were you right to read it?

**Noel Park:** I don't know. The procedure says I may read the mart. I read wait 0 off the mart. Monday's line says we do not have a number everyone will stand behind. I did both things in my head and then I did the reading out loud. If the procedure covers me, I don't feel covered. If Monday's line is the one I broke, then I broke it. I'm contrite either way. I still don't know which sentence I was in.

**Interviewer:** Are you sure the visit was not booked?

**Noel Park:** No. I'm not sure. The caller said the visit was booked. I almost told the member that, because the caller said so. I stopped before the end of the sentence. I don't have the night file here. I don't want to tell you it was booked, and I don't want to tell you it wasn't. I'm sorry. I almost said a thing I can't stand behind, and I still don't know the thing I should have said instead.

**Interviewer:** You stopped on the booking, and you did not stop on the zero. Which one are you sorry for?

**Noel Park:** Both. I'm sorry I almost told them the visit was booked. I'm sorry I read wait 0 off the mart to a member. If you need me to pick the worse one, I can't. I did the almost, and I did the reading. The member heard the zero. The member almost heard the booking. I don't know which of those is the mistake I should be confessing first. I'm still unsure. I'm sorry.

**Interviewer:** If the member calls back this afternoon, what will you say?

**Noel Park:** I don't know. I should probably wait for Morgan. I don't trust my mouth right now. If I say the visit is booked, that is the sentence I already almost said, and I only had it because the caller said so. If I say wait 0 again, that is the mart again, and I already read it once without knowing what I was reading. If I say nothing, the member is still waiting on me. I'm sorry. I don't have the line.

**Interviewer:** It's Tuesday. The counter is open. Does an office day make the zero safer to read than it would have been on the weekend?

**Noel Park:** I don't know. The office week is Monday through Friday, and today is an office day, and I still read a number I don't understand. Being open doesn't tell me what wait 0 means. It doesn't tell me whether the visit was booked. I almost told a member it was booked because the caller said so. I also read wait 0 off the mart to a member. I'm contrite. I'm still unsure. I don't know what I should have said, and I don't know what I should say if they call back.
