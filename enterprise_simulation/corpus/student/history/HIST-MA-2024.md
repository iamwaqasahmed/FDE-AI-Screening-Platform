From: Chris Pell, Finance
Place: Vancouver
Date: 16 December 2026
Subject: what is actually in the folder for contract MA-2024

I am writing this the morning after I found a sheet that was not where the folder said it would be. I want the finding on paper while the papers are still in the order I left them. This memorandum describes the sheets. It is not a billing mark. I have not written on any of them a figure that I intend someone to charge.

The contract is MA-2024, between this desk and Harbor Coverage. The jacket has lived in the finance cabinet, third drawer, since the summer of 2024. I have the parties from the face of the jacket and from the first typed text: the Vancouver desk, and Harbor Coverage. I am not retelling the whole negotiation. I am telling what a person can read if they take the folder out today, 16 December, and turn the pages in the order they are clipped.

Version 1 is the text I have always known as the body of the contract. The pages say the office week is Monday through Friday. They say the currency of record for members in British Columbia is CAD. The fee clause on those pages is 12 CAD. I read those three statements off the pages this morning, and I read them again after I had looked at the other sheets, so that I would not be remembering the fee from habit. Monday through Friday is printed as the office week. CAD is printed as the currency of record for BC members. Twelve Canadian dollars is the fee those pages name. The typing of that version is the early summer of 2024. The jacket also holds the earlier notes from April of that year, when the desk and the payer each recorded that the contract existed. I am not confusing those opening notes with the version 1 pages. The opening notes name the contract and the parties. The version 1 pages are where the week, the currency, and the fee of 12 CAD are set out as I have just written them.

In August an amendment was made. I am calling it an amendment because that is the word on the sheet, not because I have decided what force the sheet has. The sheet changes a fee from 12 CAD to 14 CAD. The line is plain enough when you are looking for it. It says the fee moves from twelve to fourteen, in CAD. The same August sheet also carries the office week as Monday through Friday, and it carries CAD as the currency of record for BC members. I write that because I saw it, not because I am treating the repetition as a comfort. A sheet can repeat a week and a currency and still be a sheet I only found in December. The date on the August amendment is an August date. It was not in my hand in August. I am not able to say, from this cabinet, who held it between August and now.

The printed packet is a different object, and I want it kept distinct from the August sheet in any later reading of this note. The packet was put together for the shelf in September. The cover of that packet is labeled version 2. I have looked at the cover and at the first page inside, and then at the fee page, and they are not the same generation of paper. The cover says version 2. The pages bound inside are the version 1 pages. The week inside is Monday through Friday. The currency inside, for BC members, is CAD. The fee inside is 12 CAD. Someone who trusted the cover would think they were holding the later text. Someone who read the fee page would be holding the first text. Both of those someones would be describing this one packet, and both descriptions would be fair to the part they touched. I have not taken the packet apart. The staple is the staple it had when I drew it out of the drawer. I do not propose to re-staple it under a different cover, and I do not propose to correct the cover with a pen this morning.

I found the August amendment in December. Yesterday, 15 December, it was in a sleeve at the back of the jacket that I do not remember as part of the September packet. The sleeve is thin, and the sheet is a single leaf, not a bound packet. Harbor Coverage logged, on their side, that an amendment packet for MA-2024 had been noted. Ruth Alvarez's office is where that logging sits. I have her logging as a fact that a packet was logged. I do not have her logging as a reading of which pages were inside the thing she logged, and I have not telephoned her to ask her to choose. I came upon our leaf on the 15th. I am writing on the 16th so the date of the finding is not left to memory. The August date on the leaf and the December date of my finding are both going to matter to anyone who later asks when we knew. I can answer when I knew. I knew yesterday.

I laid the three things on the desk in this order, left to right, and I am writing the order down so it can be rebuilt.

On the left, the version 1 pages as they have lived in the jacket since 2024. Office week Monday through Friday. Currency of record for BC members, CAD. Fee 12 CAD.

In the middle, the September printed packet. Cover labeled version 2. Inside, those same version 1 pages, fee still printed as 12 CAD, week and currency as on the left-hand pile. The cover and the inside do not agree about which version a reader is holding. I have not "fixed" that disagreement.

On the right, the August amendment, found in December. The fee on that leaf runs from 12 CAD to 14 CAD. The week line and the CAD line for BC members are on that leaf as well. The leaf is not bound into the middle packet. It was not hiding behind the version 2 cover. It was in the sleeve.

I then looked for a mark that would tell a billing clerk which of these piles to count. There is a pencil scrawl on the jacket flap, not in my hand, that speaks as if the first text had been overtaken. The pencil is undated. It does not identify the packet, and it does not identify the August leaf. I am not taking up the pencil. I am not erasing it either. It stays as someone else's mark, and this memorandum does not adopt it. There is no tick beside 12 and there is no tick beside 14. There is no stamp that says the September packet is the copy to charge from, and there is no stamp that says the August leaf is the copy to charge from. I looked twice, including the back of the August leaf and the inside cover of the packet.

Dana, I am putting a copy of this note on your shelf because the jacket is a collaborative contract and finance should not be the only room that knows the folder is in this state. The note is a description of the sheets as they sit this morning. The Monday-through-Friday line is on the version 1 pages. CAD for BC members is on the version 1 pages. The August amendment changes the fee from 12 CAD to 14 CAD. The printed packet labeled version 2 still has the version 1 pages inside it. I found the amendment in December. I have left the question of a billing copy where I found it, which is to say I have not marked one.

I have left the folder assembled, not "cleaned up." The version 1 pages are where they were. The September packet is where it was, cover still labeled version 2, inside pages still the earlier ones. The August leaf is now in front of the sleeve rather than behind it, with a paper corner dated 15 December in my hand, so the next person sees when it surfaced. I did not write 12 or 14 on that corner. I did not write a version number on that corner. The corner says only that I found the leaf, and the date.

Copies of this memorandum: the jacket itself, on top of the three piles; Dana Okonkwo's shelf; my own finance file. I am not sending it to Harbor this morning. They have whatever they logged on the 15th. If their logged packet and our sleeve turn out to be different paper, that will be a later note. I will not write that later note until I have both papers. I will not, in that later note or in this one, pretend the cover of a packet is the same thing as the pages the staple holds.

The office is on its ordinary week. I mention the week only because the contract text mentions it, and because today, a Wednesday, is a day the desk is open. I am not using the week line to lean on either fee. A contract can say Monday through Friday and still leave me with two fees on the desk. It does. Twelve is on the version 1 pages and inside the packet whose cover is labeled version 2. Fourteen is on the August leaf I found in December. Both numbers are CAD. I have put no ring around either number.
