## Why the cursor note is not the job row

Evan says that is not the job row. A cursor note tells him where she was looking. The job row is the stored row. Where someone was looking is a report about a reader. He can believe she set a cursor, as her note says, and still not have the destination, the status, the reviewer value, and the time string that the row holds. Belief that a cursor was set is not a substitute for those fields.

He had already written, on 16 January, the objects that would not count. A summary spoken in the hallway is not the job row. A screen line that mentions REF-4417 is screen text.
