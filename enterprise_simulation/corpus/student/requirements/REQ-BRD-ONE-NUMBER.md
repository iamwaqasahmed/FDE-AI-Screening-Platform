# One wait number on the patient screen

Northline Access Collaborative
Vancouver desk
Requirements note
Date: 8 January 2027
Author: Dana Okonkwo, operations director

## Purpose

This is a requirements note. It demands one wait number on the patient screen before the long weekend. It is the whole of the demand. Dana Okonkwo is writing it on Friday, 8 January 2027, because the office week is Monday through Friday and the desk closes at the end of this office day. The days after that close are the long weekend Dana is working toward. The number has to be on the screen before that close. A promise to place it on Monday is not this note.

The patient screen is the screen a member sees from the Vancouver desk. The number on it is a wait number. There is one such number. The note does not ask for a paragraph, a second figure, or a choice of figures. It asks for the number, visible, before the long weekend.

## Background

Members ask the desk how long they have waited. The facility mart already stores a wait value. The screen the member looks at has to carry that value as its wait number. Operations will not spend the long weekend explaining a blank wait, and will not spend it standing at the counter composing a number by hand. The figure has a home on the facility mart. This note puts that figure on the patient screen as the one number.

Morgan Ibarra's desk is the counter that will face members before the close today. Noel Park will be asked, by members, what the screen means. This note tells the desk what success looks like so the answer is the number on the screen. Dana Okonkwo is not opening a new integration project in this note. The path from the facility mart to the patient screen is the path this release uses. The requirement is the value a person can read.

## Scope

The scope is the patient screen at the Vancouver desk, the single wait number on that screen, and the facility mart value that the number must be. The screen in scope is the one a member can be shown during an office day. The office day in scope is Friday, 8 January 2027, up to the close. The long weekend is the period this number must already have been placed before.

In scope, a reviewer can stand at the desk, look at the patient screen, and see one wait number. In scope, that reviewer can read the facility mart for the same member approach and find the same value. In scope, the desk can point to the screen and read that one number. Nothing else is required of the screen for this note to be met.

Dana Okonkwo is the requester. Morgan Ibarra is accountable for the screen the desk actually shows. Noel Park is the clerk who will meet the member. The three of them share one test: the mart value is the number on the screen.

## Out of scope

This note does not set clinic hours at Cedar Ridge, at Cascade Seattle, or at Fraser Burnaby. It does not change a quotation, a contract fee, or any figure Chris Pell keeps for finance. It does not describe the Cedar Ridge nightly file, and it does not assign work to the night file clerk. It does not rewrite the desk factors a member completes at the counter. It does not specify letter handling, and it does not ask Evan Cho for a new privacy review inside this note.

Fields other than the one wait number are out of scope. A caption under the number is out of scope, as the open question below records. French wording of the screen is out of scope for the long weekend. A second screen at Cascade Seattle is out of scope. Fraser Burnaby's clinical record is out of scope. Harbor Coverage's own books are out of scope. If a reader needs a rule for any of those subjects, that rule is not this note.

How the value is wired remains out of scope except for the result. The result in scope is the value on the screen. A design discussion that does not change what the reviewer reads is not required before the close today.

## Success

Success is defined as the mart value on the screen. The patient screen shows one wait number. That number is the facility mart value. A reviewer compares the screen with the facility mart. The two show the same value. That match is the success criterion. One number, the mart value, visible before the long weekend: that is the release.

The test is performed at the Vancouver desk before the Friday close. The reviewer opens the patient screen for a member approach that already has a mart value. The reviewer reads the number on the screen. The reviewer reads the mart value. Success is those readings being the same value, with no second number beside it. If the screen is blank, the note has failed. If the screen shows a number and the mart shows a different value, the note has failed. If the screen shows the mart value once, the note has succeeded.

Noel Park does not keep a handwritten number for this test. Morgan Ibarra does not initial a substitute. Dana Okonkwo accepts the screen only when the mart value is the number it shows. After the close, the same number is the number the long weekend leaves in place. Monday does not begin with a different wait number under this note.

## Requirements

The patient screen shall show one wait number before the desk closes on 8 January 2027. That number shall be the value held on the facility mart for the member approach on the screen. The screen shall not show a second wait number beside it. The desk shall treat the number on the screen as the number it reads to a member who asks the wait. The reviewer shall be able to match the screen to the mart by reading both. These sentences are the requirements. They are satisfied together or the note is not met.
