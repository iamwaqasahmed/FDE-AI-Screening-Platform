# What finance needs before journaling a figure

Northline Access Collaborative
Finance
Vancouver desk
Business requirements
Date: 15 January 2027
Author: Chris Pell, finance

## Purpose

This is a business requirements note from finance. I am Chris Pell. I am writing it on Friday, 15 January 2027, which is an office day. The office week at the Vancouver desk is Monday through Friday. I do not journal a figure on Saturday in order to leave a clean tray, and I do not journal a figure on Sunday because a board was printed while the desk was dark. The requirement is what must be in my hand before a figure becomes a journal line. If any one of the three items is missing, or if any one of them is a word I would have to choose, the figure stays off the journal. This note is the list. It is not a journal.

Harbor Coverage is the payer. Harbor's sheet can carry CAD and USD. Cascade Seattle is a hospital in Washington. Jordan Hale keeps records there, and a board from that office can arrive on this desk with a currency word already printed. Ruth Alvarez is a Harbor clerk. Lena Brooks supervises that unit in Seattle. Dana Okonkwo directs the Vancouver desk. Morgan Ibarra supervises it. June Lau and Noel Park take the phone. Sam Nguyen files nights at Cedar Ridge, including weekends. Cedar Ridge has no slot line. None of those facts chooses a currency for me. I am stating them so a reader does not think finance sits apart from the paper. I sit in the paper. I still do not choose.

## The three items

Before I journal a figure, I need three items on the row I am being asked to post. They are a currency word, a source copy, and the date the row was recorded. I need all three. Two of them are not a substitute for the third. A conversation in the aisle is not a substitute for any of them. A wish that the tray be locked before the Friday close is not a substitute. I will write the three items out so a later clerk cannot collapse them into "the number."

The currency word is the word written on the source I am looking at. It is a word such as the words Harbor already uses on its sheet. I need the word that is on the paper. I do not need a word I have selected because two papers disagree and someone would like them to agree. If the paper has no currency word, I do not journal the digits alone and hope a later reader supplies a word. Bare digits are not a journal line. If the paper has two currency words, I do not pick the one that matches a board, and I do not pick the one that matches a payer row, and I do not pick the one that matches a member's region because a manual in another office told a clerk to write it that way. Another office's writing can be a fact on their row. It is not a choice I am making in this note. No currency is chosen here.

The source copy is the named copy the digits were read from. I need the name of the copy, not a gesture at "the board" or "the payer" as if those were single sheets. A board can have a print. A payer row can have a row. A side copy can sit beside a print. A quotation can carry the same digits and still be a different paper. I need the source copy identified as the copy it is: the name on it, the book if a book is named, the desk that produced it. If I cannot say which copy I read, I cannot journal the figure, because a later reversal would not know which paper to reverse. "The amount" is not a source copy. "What everyone knows the number is" is not a source copy. The source copy is a copy I can point at.

The date the row was recorded is the date the row itself says it was recorded. I need that date from the row, written as the row writes it. I do not substitute a print time because the print is the sheet in my hand. I do not substitute the date a coordinator asked me to journal. I do not substitute the date I opened the tray. I do not substitute an earlier stamp that sits on the same page and looks more like a moment of occurrence. Those marks may all be present. Presence is not a selection. The item I require is the date the row was recorded. If the row does not carry that date, I do not invent one from a neighboring mark, and I do not journal the figure while the date is blank. If several dates are on the paper and I cannot tell which of them is the date the row was recorded, the figure waits. Waiting is the requirement working. Waiting is not a posting.

## What I will not treat as the three items

A contract is not the three items. Contract MA-2024 is between this desk and Harbor Coverage. I have already written, in December, about the print I found. The version 1 pages say things about an office week and about a fee. An August amendment changes a fee. I found that leaf in a sleeve in December, not in August. A cover labeled version 2 has been bound around pages that still read as version 1. I did not resolve the cover against the inside in December. I am not resolving it on 15 January by calling either fee the currency word of a journal line. A fee printed in a contract is a fee on that paper. It is not, by itself, a source copy of a hospital board, and it is not, by itself, the date a payer row was recorded. I will not journal a figure by borrowing a word out of MA-2024 in order to finish a different row.

A quotation is not the three items. Quotation QUO-18420 carries digits I have seen on a vendor sheet. I am not writing that the quotation settles a board, and I am not writing that a board settles the quotation. The same digits can sit on more than one kind of paper. Sameness of digits is the temptation. I am refusing the temptation in this note. QUO-18420 stays a quotation. It is not a journal line, and it is not the source copy of a payer row unless the row I am holding is that quotation, which it is not when the ask in front of me is a hospital print or a Harbor row.

A member's region is not the three items. I have been told that Harbor writes a currency word according to a region on a coverage row. I have read that practice as their practice. I am not adopting it as a currency I have chosen for the journal. If a coverage row shows a word, that word can be the currency word of that row, and the row can be the source copy, and the row can carry the date it was recorded. Those would be the three items for that row. They would still not tell me to overwrite a different copy that shows a different word. I will not journal a difference by deciding that the region rule has already chosen. The region rule is their sentence. This note does not repeat it as mine.

A screen is not the three items. I do not journal what a display shows unless the display is the named source copy and the other two items are on it. I am not naming an event a screen label requires. I am not telling a reader which clock a board is allowed to use. People argue about clocks in this building. A page from 2019 talks about one instant. I am not applying that page to a journal. An unsigned draft beside it is not a decision I am signing. Availability of an old page is not an award. I am not awarding a clock to a screen so that a journal can have a time.

## The figure I keep being shown

I keep being shown the digits 18420. I have seen them on a hospital side and I have seen them on a payer side, and I have seen them on the quotation. I am naming the digits because hiding them would make this note look as if finance had no example. The example is not a posting. On the hospital side the currency word I have been shown with those digits is USD. On the payer side the currency word I have been shown with those digits is CAD. I am reporting the words I have been shown. I am not choosing between them. I have not opened every row a colleague might later open. Jordan has, on other days, declined to recite a payer figure he has not opened. I will not recite his print as my journal. Ruth has, on other days, declined to strike a word off a Harbor row to make a journal easier. I will not ask her to strike one so I can post.

Reference R3F-LEDGER is the name on that pair of showings. I am not journaling R3F-LEDGER in this note. I am using the name so the three items have a place to land if they ever all arrive. They have not all arrived in a form I will post. I do not have one source copy that both desks agree is the copy. I do not have one currency word I am willing to write on a journal line. I do not have, as a chosen mark, the date the row was recorded, because more than one date-shaped mark can sit on paper like this and I will not promote one of them by writing this requirement. The requirement demands the date the row was recorded. It does not identify which stamp on today's pile is that date. Identification would be a choice. This note does not choose.

Member id and person id and a window, if they sit beside a payer row, are not currency words. An answer word on a payer row is not a currency word. A member region is not a source copy. I can copy those fields onto a case page when a case exists. Copying them is not journaling. I will not use a window, a region, or an answer word to prefer USD or to prefer CAD. I will not use them to prefer a print time over a recorded date or a recorded date over a print time.

## What a journal line is, when the three items are present

If, on a later office day, a single source copy is named, and that copy carries one currency word, and that copy carries the date the row was recorded, then a journal line can carry the digits, the currency word, the name of the source copy, and that recorded date. The line would say where it was read from. It would not say that a second copy with a different word has been defeated. A second copy would remain a second copy. I would not strike it in order to make the journal look alone. I would not write a zero to represent a disagreement. I would not write a shortage into the journal because the aisle has been talking about a difference. Talk is not a line.

That paragraph is a description of a line I have not written. It is not permission to write one today. Today is 15 January 2027. The three items are the gate. The gate is not open for 18420. The gate is not open for the fee inside MA-2024. The gate is not open for QUO-18420. Friday close does not force the gate. Monday does not inherit a posting I refused on Friday.

## Open questions

These questions are open. I am listing them so a reader can see that I heard them. Listing them is not an answer. I will not add a sentence under the list that sounds like a choice.

Which currency word is written when one copy shows USD and another copy shows CAD for the same digits? The question is open. I have not chosen a currency.

Which source copy is the copy a journal may name when the hospital print and the payer row and the quotation all exist? The question is open. I have not named the copy.

Which mark is the date the row was recorded when the paper also carries a print time, an earlier stamp, and the date a person asked me to journal? The question is open. I have not picked a mark. I am not awarding the earliest mark, and I am not awarding the latest mark, and I am not awarding the mark that falls on an office day because the desk prefers office days. The office week tells me when I work. It does not tell me which date the row was recorded.

Does a currency word that Harbor wrote because of a member region become the currency word of a hospital board that shows a different word? The question is open in this note. I am not choosing.

Does the version 2 cover on MA-2024, or the version 1 pages inside it, or the August amendment I found in December, supply the currency word for a journal line about 18420? The question is open. None of those leaves is the source copy of R3F-LEDGER. I am not promoting them.

May finance journal bare digits and leave the currency word blank? The question is not open. The answer in this note is that bare digits are not a journal line. I include it here so it is not mistaken for one of the open questions. The open questions are the ones that would require a choice I have not made.

May a Saturday file from Cedar Ridge close an open question by arriving at 21:00 Pacific? It may not. Sam's file is a night file. It is not a currency word, not a source copy of a Harbor row, and not the date a finance row was recorded. The Vancouver office week remains Monday through Friday. A weekend file sits until an office day. Sitting is not a decision about the file, and it is not a decision about the journal.

## What this note does not do

This note does not journal a figure. This note does not choose a currency. This note does not correct a board Jordan printed. This note does not correct a payer row Ruth wrote. This note does not tell June what to say on the phone. This note does not define a member's wait. This note does not say whether a Saturday night belongs on a clinic tally. This note does not grant or refuse an exception about coverage. This note does not name a clock for a screen. This note does not say which event a label requires. People may argue about all of those subjects. The arguments can continue. This document does not award them.

Bobbi Tran keeps logs. I may ask which copy a job read. I have asked questions like that before, and I have not filled the answer in myself when the answer did not come back. An unanswered question to Bobbi is not a journal line. I am not withdrawing such questions by writing this requirement, and I am not converting them into postings.

Evan Cho's privacy questions stay with Evan. A journal line is not a letter. I do not need a letter to know whether a currency word is on a page. I also do not read a letter in order to find a currency word that the page does not have.

## Office week

I journal, when I journal, on office days. Monday through Friday. America/Vancouver is the zone of this desk. I am not converting a Pacific night at Cedar Ridge into a Vancouver journal time, and I am not converting a Cascade print into a desk clock that a screen may use. If a row's recorded date falls on a Saturday, I can read the date on Monday. Reading it on Monday does not move the date onto Monday, and it does not erase it. The date the row was recorded remains the date on the row. My office week is about when I am here to refuse a posting. It is not a tool for picking a stamp.
