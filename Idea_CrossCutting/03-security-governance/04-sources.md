## Disagreements the sources do not resolve

- **Filters.** LLM01 includes filtering and denies fool-proof prevention. The cheat sheet says screening is not a boundary. NIST AI 600-1 lists filtering as one action and still describes prompt injection as a live risk. ISO 42001 and ISO 27001 are silent on prompt filters.
- **Clock start.** Cluster notice timestamp versus PDPL awareness, plus a possible data-subject notice and an NCA report.
- **Cross-border.** Cluster hard deny into `zone-public` versus PDPL Art. 29 conditional permission. ECC cloud controls speak to classification and separation, not to a ban on external models.
- **Logs.** Destruction and data-subject deletion versus Art. 18 retention, ECC event logs, and ISO/IEC 27001 logging.
- **Kind of document.** A vulnerability list, a voluntary framework under revision, two management-system standards, a privacy statute, and a cybersecurity baseline do not stack into one "pass."
