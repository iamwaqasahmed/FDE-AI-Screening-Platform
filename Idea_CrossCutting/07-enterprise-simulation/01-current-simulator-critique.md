# Critique of the current Al-Waha simulator

The simulator is already a closed world with a public surface. `python -m alwaha.worldbuild` writes the population, exports, and corpus from one seed. `python -m alwaha.serve` runs stateful mocks. `python -m alwaha.check` scores a submission against gold and returns pass or fail without returning the answer. Practice seed `20270110` is about 60,000 people from 8 January 2023 through 31 December 2026. Assessment, team, and personal profiles shift or shrink that same generator. The reference Patient Access Assistant is one stack that can pass. Checks grade the assistant contract and the mocks' end state, so another stack can pass.

That combination is rare in the simulators reviewed in [02](02-reference-simulators.md). The evolution in [03](03-proposed-simulation-architecture.md) should add friction inside this shape.

## What is already strong

**Causes are generated, then hidden.** People, demand, capacity, behavior, infrastructure, and scripted threats are layers in the facilitator design. Students meet rows, documents, interviews, and live responses. Pairwise same-person labels, point-in-time clusters, and the attendance model's uplift sit in truth. A false merge costs more than a missed link. Lesson prose cites stable ids and treats counts and dates as measurements.

**Policies already conflict, on a clock.** Criteria v3 is in force when the engagement opens. V4 is a draft. On `rel-m03-l03` it becomes in force for endocrinology, ophthalmology, and cardiology only. An unauthorized Ramadan circular is filed as public. A Safra facility document shortens a booking window and loses to the cluster policy. In-flight referrals keep the criteria version they started with. Leaflets have a superseded twin. The 2023 minutes contain causes the postmortem omits. `dms` emits `document.changed`. Citation checks already require document id, version, status, audience, and span.

**Partners already misbehave on a schedule.** HL7 feeds replay, delay, and duplicate. Ain accepts a file and answers real-time booking with a queued state. Insurance is a stale snapshot, then an outage on `rel-m04-l04`. National identity is rate-limited, and death and id-replacement events arrive late. SMS receipts are late and duplicated. Consent can serve an expired grant from cache. WhatsApp enforces template and session-window rules. Fault switches are deterministic when a test pins the case clock. Weekend, Ramadan hours, and Eid closure are part of the SLA calendar, so a hardcoded Gregorian offset fails the assessment seed.

**Identity is already the hard problem.** The generator plants several English spellings of one Arabic name, a shared family phone, a newborn under the mother's national id, a visitor number later replaced by an Iqama, a reused MRN at Nakhl, a Safra merge that was applied and reversed, twins, a late death flag, check-digit failures, and a Hijri date stored as `YYYY-MM-DD` with no era marker. Wadi's FHIR clinic code changed from `END-01` to `ENDO-CLN` while the catalog still defines the old code. Students are scored on clusters, not on MRN equality.

**Procurement is already a date collision.** Finance copies a 16-week GPU purchase lead time onto the price sheet. Renting is available in days. A purchase started for a 10 January 2027 engagement misses the 8 February 2027 sponsor date. The change calendar includes a Ramadan freeze. Layla Al-Harbi (`cdo`) states the go-live first and reveals the 16-week assumption only if asked. Reem Al-Subaie (`finance`) reveals rent versus buy on a follow-up. The interview tool walks a scripted tree and does not print a score.

**Observability is already partial.** Seventeen exports each carry one planted defect. Ain is a nightly file. The patient API hides `pending` until a product change. PHC templates still emit v3. The BI mart starts the clock at `booking_date` and counts people as rows. Staff behavior (rubber-stamping, shorthand, approval latency) is generated from named parameters and surfaces through personas and artifacts. Demos print an effect and withhold the cause.

**The grader is already an outcome checker.** Foundation checks cover repository hygiene, interviews, events, agent end state, trajectories, FHIR journeys, identity precision and recall, citation tuples, SLO numbers, and required fields on DPIA, model-card, and runbook templates. Simpler methods are required to lose named slices, including an uncalibrated model judge (`slice-judge`). The corpus pipeline may let a model phrase a document, then a checker rejects a missing or extra fact. The published pack is the frozen artifact.

## Where the six frictions are still thin

The pieces exist. Several of them are lessons or switches, rather than a standing property of every session.

**Versioned policy is a corpus fact more than a service behavior.** Students can read Markdown, a PDF, or a scan. The design gives `dms` a search and a change feed. It does not describe a history a client can walk the way a FHIR server walks `_history`: every prior hash, including drafts and the unauthorized circular, returned as data. A team can still treat "the file named v4" or the top search hit as the rule. The binding function (in force, inside the effective window, site scope, cluster beats an illegal facility narrowing, in-flight work keeps its start version) is gold. The live service should make the wrong version easy to retrieve and the right version a conclusion.

**Partner failure is pinned to release ids.** Replay, the insurance outage, and the consent-cache switch teach specific lessons. Between those lessons a partner can look reliable. Unreliable partners in an enterprise are boring and continuous: the maps call for `phc-rim` times out, a receipt arrives twice next week, eligibility is 48 hours old on an ordinary Tuesday. The case clock can still drive a fully determined script. The script should be denser than the lesson switches, and it should stay unpublished.

**Bilingual content is a person defect, not a document system.** Arabic appears as spellings of names and as Hijri dates. The corpus families (criteria, leaflets, hours, safety, privacy, vendor proposals) are specified without a language tag. A phone-only patient and a caregiver are in the adoption model. The documents those people would be sent are not specified as an Arabic rendering and an English rendering of the same facts, with one rendering stale. Identity work then under-practices the actual ambiguity: an official Arabic name, a transliteration, and a Latin spelling in another hospital are one person, and a shared phone is a family.

**Identity gold is well designed, and the student still needs a fractured view.** The generator knows the canonical person. The teaching value is that no export is that person. The risk in a later "upgrade" is a single FHIR `Patient` bundle per human, which collapses Nakhl's MRN, Safra's reversed merge, and the national-id timeline into one resource. Module 3 checks (`chk-m03-l01-identity`) only mean something if the pack stays fractured and the labels stay in `truth/`.

**Procurement delay is a constant on a note.** Sixteen weeks and the freeze are visible once the student finds the procurement note or asks finance. There is no partner that accepts a purchase order, acknowledges it late, ships part of an order, and then stops for the freeze. Students can write "we will buy GPUs" in prose and sound plausible. A deterministic check needs a field (`gpu_path`, `ready_date`) compared with the case clock, plus a status feed whose lag is part of the world. The beer-game property to add is local vision: the team sees its own order, not the vendor's queue.

**Partial observability is planted, then sometimes copied forward.** Price keys in truth are copied onto the finance export the team receives. That is right for a price sheet. It is wrong for identity, policy binding, fault scripts, canaries, scan transcriptions, and attendance parameters. Some effects are single planted rows. A stronger partial view is structural: each system publishes what it will never return, what it returns late, and what it returns under the wrong code. The joined person file never ships.

## What must not be "fixed"

The narrative quality is the product. Twelve personas with a must-surface list and a must-not-claim list, workshops whose dot-vote disagrees with the value model, a vendor who cites another population, and demos that refuse to name the cause are doing work a generic synthetic EHR does not do. The reference architecture (gateway, intake, approvals, waitlist, router, private model, external model) stays held back. Checks stay on the contract in the live-services note: `POST /v1/chat`, approvals, health with model hash, and the resulting mock state.

Small generator modules, stable ids, fictional clinical boundaries, and release packs that can invalidate an earlier design are the engineering constraints. A thicker simulation that needs a hosted cloud account, a public FHIR sandbox, or a live model to grade a lesson is a step down from the laptop the course already runs.
